How Property Tax Is Calculated

Property tax in India is levied by local municipal corporations (such as BBMP in Bangalore, BMC in Mumbai, or GHMC in Hyderabad) to fund urban infrastructure, sanitation, and municipal services.

Step 1
Determine Assessment Value
Calculate base annual value using Unit Area Value (UAV = Area x Rate per sq.ft) or Annual Rental Value (ARV).
Step 2
Apply Assessment Multipliers
Multiply by age/depreciation factor, occupancy usage factor (self-occupied vs rented), and location zone factor.
Step 3
Calculate Base Tax & Cess
Apply the municipal property tax rate (e.g., 20%), then add solid waste, health, and education cess.
Step 4
Apply Early Payment Rebates
Deduct applicable early-bird rebates (typically 5% for payment within the initial quarter) to get net tax payable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is property tax calculated in Indian municipalities?

Property tax is typically calculated using either the Unit Area Value (UAV) method (Area x Rate per sq.ft x Depreciation/Usage factors x Tax Rate) or the Annual Rental Value (ARV) method.

What is the difference between built-up area and carpet area for property tax?

Municipalities like BBMP calculate tax based on total built-up area (including walls and balconies), whereas some authorities use carpet area. Always verify your local municipal rules.

Can I get a rebate on property tax?

Yes, many municipalities (such as BBMP Bangalore) offer a 5% early-payment rebate if property tax is paid within the first quarter of the financial year.
