What Does It Really Cost to Buy a Home in Whitefield?

The quoted apartment price in Whitefield micro-markets (EPIP Zone, ITPL Main Road, Varthur Road, Hope Farm) is only the base starting point. Government taxes, builder deposits, and interior fitouts materially expand your required capital budget.

Layer 1
Quoted Base Price
The basic rate per sq. ft. multiplied by super built-up saleable area.
Layer 2
Government & Tax Charges
Karnataka Stamp Duty (~5%), Registration Fee (~1%), and GST (5% for under-construction).
Layer 3
Project & Builder Charges
Covered parking, clubhouse membership, sinking fund corpus, legal fees, and advance maintenance.
Layer 4
Interior & Move-in Budget
Modular kitchen, wardrobes, lighting, false ceiling, and electrical appliances needed to make the property liveable.

Whitefield Project Cost Checklist Before Booking

  • ✓ Demand an itemized all-in cost sheet from the builder before paying any booking token.
  • ✓ Verify whether parking (covered basement/open) is included in the base price or billed separately.
  • ✓ Confirm if GST is applicable and check whether the property has received an Occupancy Certificate (OC).
  • ✓ Check carpet area vs saleable area ratio to verify actual usable living space.
  • ✓ Verify current Karnataka stamp duty and Kaveri registration fees on Kaveri Online Services Portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much extra does it cost to buy an apartment in Whitefield over the quoted price?

Additional acquisition charges (stamp duty, registration, GST, legal fees, clubhouse, parking, and maintenance deposits) typically add 6% to 15% over the quoted base property price in Whitefield.

What is the difference between Quoted Price per sq. ft. and Effective Price per sq. ft.?

Quoted Price per sq. ft. is base property price divided by area. Effective Price per sq. ft. includes all government taxes, builder charges, and registration fees divided by the selected area.

Are interiors included in the Total Acquisition Cost?

No. Total Acquisition Cost includes only property purchase price, government taxes, legal fees, and builder deposits. Interior fitout costs are calculated separately under Total Move-in Cost.
