What is a Builder Trust Score?

The Builder Trust Score evaluates multiple dimensions of a real estate developer's track record to help prospective homebuyers look beyond marketing promises. Evaluating experience, execution speed, finishing quality, RERA compliance, financial health, and litigation history provides a holistic view of builder reliability.

Step 1
Track Record & Delivery
Evaluates years in business, total completed projects, and historic on-time delivery rates.
Step 2
Build Quality & Feedback
Assesses structural finishing standards, defect rates, and verified customer review scores.
Step 3
Legal & Financial Governance
Checks RERA compliance, financial stability, after-sales service, and active litigation risk.
Step 4
Due-Diligence Guidance
Generates actionable recommendations and flags critical risk areas for independent verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Builder Trust Score calculated?

The Builder Trust Score (out of 100) evaluates nine weighted parameters: Experience (10%), Track Record (10%), On-Time Delivery (15%), Construction Quality (15%), Customer Satisfaction (15%), Legal Compliance (10%), Financial Strength (10%), After-Sales Service (10%), and Litigation Risk Adjustment (5%).

What do the Trust Classifications mean?

Scores between 80-100 indicate EXCELLENT trust, 65-79 indicate STRONG trust, 50-64 indicate MODERATE trust, and 0-49 indicate HIGHER RISK builder profile.

Does acreNkey independently verify builder quality or legal litigation?

No. The inputs in this calculator are user-provided screening values. The tool provides a structured framework to evaluate developer reliability and does not replace professional legal or technical due diligence.

What red flags trigger automatic warnings?

Automatic warning banners are displayed for Litigation Risk > 70, On-Time Delivery < 50%, Customer Satisfaction < 50, Compliance Score < 50, or Financial Strength < 40.
