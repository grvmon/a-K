How Much Can Negotiation Really Save?

Most home buyers measure negotiation success purely by the immediate discount on the quoted property price. However, when you finance your purchase with a home loan, every rupee saved on the purchase price also reduces the principal amount you borrow — creating compounding interest savings over your loan tenure.

Step 1
Reduce Property Price
Negotiate a discount from the seller or builder's quoted price.
Negotiated Price = Quoted Price × (1 − Discount %)
Step 2
Calculate Immediate Savings
Determine your direct cash savings on the acquisition cost.
Price Savings = Quoted Price − Negotiated Price
Step 3
Calculate Loan Reduction
Borrowing less money lowers your principal loan requirement.
Loan Reduction = Price Savings × LTV %
Step 4
Calculate EMI Savings
Enjoy lower monthly instalments for the entire tenure.
EMI Savings = Original EMI − New EMI
Step 5
Calculate Interest Savings
Save tens of lakhs in cumulative interest payable to the bank.
Interest Savings = Original Interest − New Interest
Step 6
Calculate Total Financial Benefit
Combine immediate price savings with long-term interest savings.
Total Benefit = Price Savings + Interest Savings
