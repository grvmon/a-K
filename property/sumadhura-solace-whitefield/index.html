About Sumadhura Solace

Sumadhura Solace (Codename E-119) is a premium residential development located on Marathahalli Main Road, Thubarahalli, Whitefield. Spread across 11.66 acres with 81% open space, the project features 7 architecturally refined towers (2B + G + 18 floors) offering 777 luxury 3 & 4 BHK residences with twin lake views and a 45,000 sq.ft. clubhouse.

AcrenKey Independent Review

Our advisory team evaluates Sumadhura Solace as a top-tier end-use residential development in the Whitefield corridor. Its 81% open space ratio and lakefront setting provide long-term lifestyle value, while tech hub proximity supports steady rental demand.
