How do you use the hidden cost calculator?

  1. Enter the agreed price and pick the property type: resale, a new ready flat, a new under-construction flat (5% GST) or an affordable home (1% GST).
  2. Set your down payment. The calculator keeps the loan within RBI limits.
  3. Add the brokerage and any other quotes you have under “Add your other costs”.
  4. The stamp duty, cess, surcharge, registration and GST are worked out for you. Open “Adjust rates” to change them.

What are the hidden costs of buying a flat in Bengaluru?

The price on the agreement is never the whole bill. On top of it come government charges, GST on new construction, and costs that depend on your deal: brokerage, legal checks, the bank’s processing fee, parking, a maintenance deposit and interiors.

The government charges are fixed by law, so the calculator works them out. The others vary too much to guess, so they start at zero and you enter your own quotes.

Worked example: ₹ 80 L under-construction flat

Stamp duty at 5% is ₹ 4,00,000, the cess ₹ 40,000, the surcharge ₹ 8,000 and registration ₹ 1,60,000: ₹ 6,08,000 in all. GST at 5% adds ₹ 4,00,000. With ₹ 20,000 for legal checks and a ₹ 15,000 loan fee, the costs on top come to ₹ 10,43,000, or 13.04% of the price.

With 25% down (₹ 20,00,000), the cash you need before the keys is ₹ 30,43,000.

Which costs can the loan cover?

None of the costs on top. Banks lend against the property’s value, within RBI limits: up to 90% for loans to ₹ 30 L, 80% to ₹ 75 L and 75% above that. Stamp duty, registration, GST on top of the price, brokerage and interiors come out of your own pocket, so plan for them with the down payment.

To see the loan interest too, use the total cost of buying a home calculator. For the monthly bill after you move in, try the monthly home ownership cost calculator.

Which buying costs apply in Bengaluru?

On a sale deed in Bengaluru, Karnataka charges 5% stamp duty on the market value (Karnataka Stamp Act, Schedule Art. 20(1)), a 10% cess on the duty (s.3B) and, inside the Greater Bengaluru Area, a 2% surcharge on the duty (Greater Bengaluru Governance Act 2024, s.170). The registration fee is 2% (IGR notification of 29 Aug 2025, in force from 31 Aug 2025). Together that is 7.6% of the value. The first sale of a flat pays less duty: 2% up to ₹ 20 L and 3% above ₹ 20 L up to ₹ 45 L, so the total is 4.24% or 5.36%. Resale flats, plots and houses pay 5% at any price. Rates verified 18 Oct 2026 · confirm on Kaveri Online before registering.

GST on an under-construction home is 5%, or 1% for an affordable home, per the CBIC real estate FAQ. If the price is ₹ 50 L or more, you deduct 1% TDS from the payment to the seller. That is not an extra cost, but you must file it.

Frequently asked questions

The hidden costs of buying a flat in Bengaluru are stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration (7.6% of the value for a resale flat), GST of 5% on an under-construction flat, and deal-specific costs such as brokerage, legal checks, the loan processing fee, parking, a maintenance deposit and interiors.

Budget at least the government charges and GST. On a ₹ 1 crore resale flat the government charges are ₹ 7,60,000; on an under-construction flat, with 5% GST, they are ₹ 12,60,000. Add your own quotes for brokerage, legal work and interiors on top.

A home loan does not usually cover stamp duty, registration or GST charged on top of the price. Banks lend a share of the property value within RBI limits, so these costs come from your own savings along with the down payment. Check with your lender for its own policy.
