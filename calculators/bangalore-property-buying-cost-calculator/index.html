What Does a Bangalore Property Really Cost?

The advertised property price is not the final figure you need to budget. Government charges, taxes, legal documentation fees, and builder-specific additions can increase the total acquisition cost by 6% to 12%.

Cost 1
Karnataka Stamp Duty
A mandatory state tax paid to validate the property transfer deed, typically ~5% of the property value.
Cost 2
Registration Fee
Charge paid to the sub-registrar office to officially record property ownership in government land records, typically 1%.
Cost 3
GST (Goods & Services Tax)
Applies to under-construction residential properties (typically 5%). Ready-to-move properties with an OC are exempt from GST.
Cost 4
Legal & Documentation
Professional advocate charges for title search, encumbrance certificate verification, and sale agreement drafting.

"Before You Buy" Bangalore Property Checklist

  • ✓ Verify Khata Certificate (A-Khata vs B-Khata) and latest Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 30 years.
  • ✓ Confirm RERA Registration number of the project on the Karnataka RERA portal.
  • ✓ Verify Occupancy Certificate (OC) status if buying a Ready-to-Move property to confirm GST exemption.
  • ✓ Review builder allotment agreement for hidden infrastructure, parking, or maintenance deposit clauses.
  • ✓ Verify exact stamp duty rates on official Kaveri Online Services Portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much additional cost should I budget over property price in Bangalore?

In Bangalore, government stamp duty (typically ~5%), registration fees (~1%), GST (5% for under-construction residential), brokerage, and legal fees generally add 6% to 12% over the quoted property price.

Is GST applicable on Ready-to-Move or Resale apartments in Bangalore?

No. GST is generally not applicable on resale properties or ready-to-move apartments that have received an Occupancy Certificate (OC).

What is the difference between Total Buying Cost and Upfront Cash Required?

Total Buying Cost is Property Price plus all Additional Purchase Charges. Upfront Cash Required is your Down Payment plus all Additional Purchase Charges.
