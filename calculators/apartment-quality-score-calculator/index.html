Why Carpet-to-Saleable Ratio & Livability Matter

A larger super built-up area does not automatically mean more usable living space. Evaluating carpet efficiency alongside natural light, cross-ventilation, floor plan usability, privacy, and noise levels helps buyers evaluate physical livability before booking an apartment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Apartment Quality Score calculated?

The Quality Score (0-100) evaluates 10 weighted parameters: Space Efficiency (15%), Floor Plan Usability (15%), Natural Light (10%), Ventilation (10%), Room Dimensions (10%), Privacy (10%), Noise Levels (10%), Construction Execution (10%), View/Floor Height (5%), and Finishing Quality (5%).

What is Carpet-to-Saleable Efficiency?

Carpet-to-Saleable Efficiency is the ratio of usable carpet area to total quoted super built-up area. Higher efficiency means a greater percentage of the purchased space converts into usable living area.

What do the Quality Classifications mean?

Scores between 85-100 indicate EXCELLENT quality, 70-84 indicate VERY GOOD quality, 55-69 indicate GOOD quality, 40-54 indicate AVERAGE quality, and 0-39 indicate POOR quality.

Does this tool replace a physical technical snagging inspection?

No. Construction and finishing scores are user-entered screening ratings. For a final purchase decision, we recommend an independent technical snagging inspection to verify structural work, plumbing, and electrical safety.
