About Sattva Songbird

Sattva Songbird is a mixed-format residential development in the Cheemasandra / Budigere Road corridor. The supplied research describes a larger community combining high-rise apartments with 4 BHK row houses, giving it a broader housing mix than a conventional apartment-only project.

The project's strongest lifestyle hooks are its reported 80% open area, broad amenity offering and multiple clubhouse zones. Its location thesis is road-led, with Budigere Road and NH75 providing onward access toward Whitefield and KR Puram.

Phase I spans approximately 12–12.1 acres (1,379 homes in primary table / 1,321 apartments + 58 row houses in detailed assessment), while Phase II spans approximately 4.3 acres (380 units). Total detailed assessment covers 6 towers and 1,758 units across 16.4 acres.

INDEPENDENT ADVISORY VERDICT

Acre&Key Verdict

Mixed-Format Living on Budigere Road

Sattva Songbird suits buyers who want broader configuration choice and a large amenity-led community, and who are comfortable taking a longer view on Budigere. The trade-offs are meaningful: high density, high-rise living, road dependence, a 2029 completion horizon and dependency on a future phase.

Our View

✓ Buy for:
Studio to 3 BHK apartment choice + 2.5 BHK option + Mixed apartment + row-house identity + 80% reported open area + Broad outdoor and indoor amenity set + Budigere Road / NH75 regional access.
! Be cautious about:
Approximately 115 Phase-I homes/acre based on supplied figures + High-rise living + Lift loading remains unverified + No close operational metro + Budigere junction / gate approach + Long delivery horizon to May 2029 + Future Phase-II/common-area dependency + Large competing Budigere pipeline.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Sattva Songbird FAQs

Essential answers for prospective buyers
What is the price of Sattva Songbird? +
The supplied apartment pricing ranges from approximately ₹0.72–0.77 Cr for studios to ₹2.44–2.67 Cr for 3 BHKs.
What configurations are available? +
The primary pricing table covers Studio, 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 2.5 BHK and 3 BHK apartments. The wider community assessment also references 4 BHK row houses.
What is the base price? +
₹12,600/sq.ft. in the supplied scheme.
What are the additional charges? +
The source reports included PLC, ₹25/sq.ft./floor floor rise from the 3rd floor, ₹4 lakh parking, included clubhouse, ₹3.45 lakh infrastructure, ₹48/sq.ft. maintenance and ₹100/sq.ft. corpus.
How far is the metro? +
Whitefield/Kadugodi Metro is reported at approximately 6.1 km.
What is the possession date? +
The supplied research states RERA completion as 6 May 2029.
Is Sattva Songbird good for end use? +
It best fits buyers comfortable with Budigere, high-rise living and a longer delivery horizon who value configuration choice, open-area claims and amenities.
Is it good for investment? +
It is more of a long-horizon corridor thesis than a near-term rental play because current maturity, future phases and competing supply remain important.
What are the biggest risks? +
High density, unverified lift loading, road dependence, 2029 delivery, future-phase rights and competing Budigere supply.
How large is the project? +
The detailed assessment describes 16.4 acres, 6 towers and 1,758 units overall, with Phase I around 12 acres and Phase II around 4.3 acres. Exact phase counts should be verified.
