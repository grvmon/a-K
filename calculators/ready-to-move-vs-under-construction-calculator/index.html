How This Decision Calculator Works & Formulas Explained expand_more

1. Cash Outflow Calculation:
RTM Cash Outflow includes purchase price plus stamp duty, registration, and taxes.
Under-Construction Cash Outflow includes purchase price, charges, taxes, plus monthly rent paid during waiting and progressive pre-EMI loan interest paid to the bank.

2. Progressive Pre-EMI Construction Interest:
Calculated assuming equal monthly bank disbursements across the construction period ($M$). For month $t$, interest is charged on the cumulative disbursed principal ($t imes ext{Disb}$).

3. Property Value at Possession:
Future property value is calculated as $ ext{Purchase Price} imes (1 + ext{Annual Appreciation})^{ ext{Years}}$. Appreciation is kept conceptually separate from cash outflow to avoid misleading headline numbers.

4. Break-Even Under-Construction Price:
The exact UC purchase price at which the net economic outcome of buying under-construction equals Ready-to-Move, accounting for rent, interest, taxes, and appreciation.

Ready-to-Move vs Under-Construction: What Are You Really Comparing?

Choosing between a ready-to-move (RTM) home and an under-construction (UC) property is one of the most critical decisions for Indian property buyers. While under-construction properties often advertise lower base prices, buyers frequently overlook the hidden cost of waiting — including monthly rent and progressive pre-EMI loan interest.

Factor 1
Property Purchase Price
Under-construction properties usually offer a 5% to 15% discount over ready homes in the same locality.
Factor 2
Rent Paid During Construction
If you rent while waiting for possession, every month's rent is an unrecoverable cash outflow added to your total cost.
Factor 3
Pre-EMI Construction Interest
Bank disbursements occur in stages. You pay monthly pre-EMI interest to the bank on disbursed funds without building home equity.
Factor 4
Capital Appreciation Upside
An under-construction property may appreciate in market value between booking and handover, building early equity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ready-to-Move always better financially?

Not always. Ready-to-Move eliminates waiting rent, construction interest, and delay risks immediately. However, if an under-construction property offers a large purchase discount and high capital appreciation during construction, it can yield a higher net economic return.

Is Under-Construction always cheaper?

No. While the advertised price of an under-construction home is often lower, adding 24 to 36 months of rent and progressive pre-EMI loan interest frequently erodes or completely reverses the headline discount.

How does construction delay affect the result?

A possession delay of 6 to 12 months directly increases your rent outgoings and pre-EMI interest payments. Every additional month of delay adds tens of thousands of rupees in unrecoverable waiting costs.

What is the break-even Under-Construction price?

The break-even UC price is the maximum price at which buying under-construction remains financially advantageous after accounting for rent, pre-EMI interest, GST, and appreciation. If the actual UC price is higher than the break-even price, Ready-to-Move is financially stronger.

Does GST apply to Ready-to-Move property?

No. GST is not applicable on completed Ready-to-Move properties that have received a Completion Certificate (CC). GST applies primarily to under-construction residential properties (typically 5% for standard housing and 1% for affordable housing).

Should investors use different assumptions from self-use buyers?

Yes. Self-use buyers prioritize avoiding current rent and securing immediate occupation. Investors should factor in immediate rental income earned from a Ready-to-Move property during the construction period versus delayed rental income from an under-construction project.
