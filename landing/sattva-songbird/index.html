About

Sattva Songbird is a mixed-format residential community on Budigere Road, combining apartments with 4 BHK row houses across a 16.4-acre overall development. Its key proposition is broad configuration choice, 80% reported open area and a large amenity ecosystem, while the location remains primarily road-dependent for everyday employment access.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
•Mixed-format community — Apartments + 4 BHK row houses
•80% reported open area — Large open-space proposition
•Broad configuration choice — Studio to 3 BHK apartments
DUE DILIGENCE
•High Phase-I density (~115 homes/acre)
•No close operational metro (~6.1 km)
•Road-dependent commute (ITPL access remains traffic-dependent)

Location & Connectivity

Budigere Main Road • NH75 • East Bengaluru Growth Corridor

Positioned on the Cheemasandra / Budigere Road corridor, Sattva Songbird benefits from direct regional-road access through Budigere Road and NH75, with onward connectivity toward Whitefield and KR Puram. The trade-off is that everyday employment commutes remain largely road-dependent, while the nearest operational metro is reported at approximately 6.1 km.

Budigere Road / NH75
