Maintenance is More Than the Headline Rate

Homebuyers often make the mistake of evaluating society maintenance based solely on the advertised per-square-foot rate (e.g. ₹5/sq. ft.). However, fixed utility charges, parking fees, clubhouse maintenance, GST, and sinking funds can increase your actual monthly bill by 25% to 60%.

Understanding the Maintenance Cost Formula

Your true monthly society maintenance bill is calculated as:

Base Maintenance = (Saleable Area × Rate) + Fixed Fees + Parking + Amenities + Water

Monthly Maintenance = Base Maintenance + Applicable GST (18%)

Recurring Annual Outflow = (Monthly Maintenance × 12) + Sinking Fund Corpus

Component Billing Method Typical Impact
Area-Based Fee ₹ / sq. ft. / month Covers security, housekeeping, common lighting, and lift maintenance.

5-Step Society Maintenance Due Diligence Checklist

Step 01
Request Maintenance Statement

Obtain an actual maintenance bill issued to a current resident of the same flat size.

Step 02
Check Sinking Fund Balances

Verify if the society association has adequate corpus reserves for elevator replacement and waterproofing.

Step 03
Verify Water Supply Source

High water tanker dependency significantly increases monthly utility surcharges during summer months.

Step 04
Confirm GST Applicability

Verify if individual flat maintenance exceeds ₹7,500/month, triggering 18% GST liability.

Step 05
Review Annual Fee Hikes

Check historical AGM minutes for standard 5% to 10% annual escalation trends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GST applicable on society maintenance?

Yes. If your monthly maintenance bill exceeds ₹7,500 per month per flat and the RWA's annual turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs, 18% GST applies on the entire maintenance amount.

What is a Corpus or Sinking Fund?

A sinking fund is collected for long-term structural repairs, building repainting, and major equipment upgrades. It is distinct from routine operational maintenance.
