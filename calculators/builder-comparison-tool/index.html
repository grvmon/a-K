Don't Compare Builders Only by Brand Name

A builder's marketing budget or brand visibility does not guarantee structural quality, financial health, or timely project delivery. Objective developer evaluation requires scoring track record evidence across 10 critical pillars.

Why Litigation Risk Adjustment Matters

A developer might possess an established 20-year delivery track record, but if their current projects suffer from severe legal disputes or land title litigations, their risk-adjusted score must reflect this vulnerability:

Risk-Adjusted Score = Trust Score × (1 − (Litigation Risk / 100))

Overall Score = 30% Trust + 20% Quality + 15% Delivery + 15% Financials + 20% Risk-Adjusted

Score Band Classification Interpretation

5-Step Developer Evaluation Framework

Step 01
Verify RERA Delivery History

Check committed RERA possession dates versus actual completion certificates issued.

Step 02
Audit Construction Workmanship

Inspect completed projects built by the same developer 3 to 5 years ago for wall cracking or water seepage.

Step 03
Check Encumbrance & Title History

Review bank tie-ups and RERA legal filings for litigation or mortgage encumbrances.

Step 04
Assess Financial Solvency

Ensure the developer relies on institutional debt or private equity funding rather than pure buyer pre-sales.

Step 05
Evaluate After-Sales Responsiveness

Talk to existing resident welfare associations (RWA) regarding warranty defect repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is on-time delivery weighted so heavily?

Project delays increase pre-EMI interest burdens and double rent expenses for homebuyers. Timely delivery is a primary indicator of developer execution capability.

What happens if I don't have financial data for a builder?

The tool automatically excludes missing metrics from calculation and re-normalizes remaining weights, adjusting the Evidence Coverage indicator.
