Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers to key buyer questions about Sumadhura Solace Whitefield.

Is Sumadhura Solace RERA approved?
Yes, Sumadhura Solace is fully RERA approved under registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/111225/008330.
Where is Sumadhura Solace located?
Sumadhura Solace is located on Marathahalli Main Road, Thubarahalli Village, Varthur Hobli, Whitefield, Bengaluru.
What is the starting price for Sumadhura Solace?
Starting prices begin at ₹2.18 Cr* for 3 BHK residences (1,865–2,050 sq.ft.) and ₹3.10 Cr* for 4 BHK luxury suites (2,450–2,680 sq.ft.).
What configurations are available at Sumadhura Solace?
Sumadhura Solace offers spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK luxury residences ranging from 1,865 sq.ft. to 2,680 sq.ft. saleable area with ~66-67% carpet efficiency.
What is the target possession date?
The target completion and handover possession date for Sumadhura Solace is December 2029.
How many units and towers are in the project?
The development comprises 7 architecturally refined towers (LUMA, SORA, VANA, NIRA, KORA, MIRA, OLEA) with 777 total residences (526 in Phase 1).
What is the total project land area?
Sumadhura Solace spans 11.66 acres with 81% open space and surrounding twin lake frontage.
Which metro station is nearest to Sumadhura Solace?
The Whitefield / Hope Farm Metro Station (Purple Line) is approximately 15 minutes away, providing seamless connectivity across Bengaluru.
Which tech parks are near Sumadhura Solace?
Major nearby IT hubs include ITPL (14 mins), Outer Ring Road ORR (5 mins), Marathahalli (12 mins), Brigade Tech Gardens, and Prestige Tech Park.
Is Sumadhura Solace good for investment?
AcrenKey rates Sumadhura Solace at 9.2/10 for investment owing to Whitefield's strong IT tenant pool, 14.3% 5-year historical CAGR, and rare lakefront positioning.
Is it suitable for end use?
Yes, with 81% open space, 45,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, 90+ amenities, and top schools nearby, it is ideally suited for families seeking long-term living.
What are the maintenance charges?
Maintenance charges will be finalized prior to handover by the builder association (typically ₹4 – ₹5 per sq.ft./month for luxury Whitefield communities).
Who is the builder behind Sumadhura Solace?
Sumadhura Group, an established developer with 30+ years of legacy, 52+ delivered projects, and over 12,000 satisfied families.
