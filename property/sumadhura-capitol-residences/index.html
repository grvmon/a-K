About Sumadhura Capitol Residences

Sumadhura Capitol Residences is positioned in the Hope Farm / ITPL Main Road micro-market as a compact 3 and 4 BHK residential community. The project's strongest proposition is its central Whitefield location, strong metro catchment, ITPL / EPIP access and relatively near-term completion.

Unlike large township-scale developments, Capitol Residences is a smaller community proposition. This can appeal to buyers who prioritise location, commute convenience and a more compact community over a very large amenity ecosystem.

The central end-use and investment question is whether the project's premium entry price is justified by its location advantage, metro proximity, product size and completion timeline.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Sumadhura Capitol Residences FAQs

Essential answers for prospective buyers
What is the price of Sumadhura Capitol Residences? +
The supplied research reports approximately ₹2.55–3.04 Cr for 3 BHK and ₹3.23–3.43 Cr for 4 BHK homes.
What configurations are available? +
3 BHK and 4 BHK.
What are the sizes? +
3 BHK: 1,635–1,890 sq.ft. | 4 BHK: 2,100–2,150 sq.ft.
What is the base price? +
₹14,500/sq.ft. before listed add-ons.
What are the additional charges? +
PLC up to ₹300/sq.ft., floor rise ₹25/sq.ft. per floor from 5th floor onward, parking ₹6.5 lakh / ₹8 lakh, clubhouse ₹3 lakh, infrastructure ₹400/sq.ft., maintenance ₹96/sq.ft. and corpus ₹100/sq.ft.
How close is the metro? +
Kadugodi Tree Park Metro is reported at approximately 180 metres.
How far is ITPL? +
ITPL and EPIP are reported within 5 km.
What is the RERA completion date? +
The supplied research reports 30 December 2027.
Is Capitol good for end use? +
It is best aligned with buyers prioritising Whitefield convenience, metro access, ITPL/EPIP proximity, 3/4 BHK family space and a relatively compact community.
Is Capitol good for investment? +
The supplied assessment highlights strong liquidity and rental/resale depth, but investors should assess the premium entry price, competing supply and traffic before buying.
What are the biggest risks? +
Premium pricing, peak-hour Hope Farm/Whitefield traffic, smaller amenity scale and unresolved unit-count/plan reconciliation.
How many units are there? +
The primary project table reports 368 units, while another part of the supplied research refers to approximately 405 homes. This should be reconciled against sanctioned/RERA documents.
