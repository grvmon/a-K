To make standard real estate photography instantly feel like high-end, editorial imagery, you want to mimic the look of an expensive camera lens and analog film. Raw digital photos often look too harsh, saturated, or sterile for a luxury brand.

### 1. The "Warm Film" Filter

I added a pseudo-element over the image that uses `mix-blend-mode: multiply` with a very subtle Deep Navy gradient at the bottom. This visually "anchors" the image into the dark UI elements and ensures that if you ever place white text over the image, it will be perfectly readable.

### 4. The Reveal Interaction
When a user hovers over the image, it executes a slow, luxurious `1.03` scale zoom, and the "Warm Film" filter fades away, revealing the raw, true colors of the photograph. It rewards the user's interaction by giving them the unfiltered view only when they show intent.
