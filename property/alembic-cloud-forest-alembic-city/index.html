About Alembic Cloud Forest

Alembic Cloud Forest is positioned as the residential component of Alembic City, an integrated live-work development in the Kadugodi / Hope Farm micro-market. The supplied research describes approximately 15 acres of residential development and 1,330 homes.

The project's core differentiation is not simply the apartment product, but its setting inside a broader Alembic City ecosystem. The source describes a nature-led, biophilic/agrihood identity, alongside strong access to Hope Farm, Whitefield and the metro network.

For buyers, the proposition is therefore a combination of location convenience, township integration and differentiated positioning. The key diligence question is whether the eventual density, shared infrastructure and delivery experience match the premium positioning.

Diligence Note: Primary pricing table references 3 towers while detailed material also references 5 towers in one section. Verify the applicable phase and tower count before booking.

LOCALITY OVERVIEW

Kadugodi / Hope Farm

A mature employment-linked location with a strong metro advantage.

Unlike many outer-corridor projects, Cloud Forest's location thesis is based on proximity rather than a distant future-connectivity story. Hope Farm and the Whitefield/Kadugodi metro network are close, while Whitefield employment access is a key demand driver.

Alembic Cloud Forest FAQs

Essential answers for prospective buyers
What is the price of Alembic Cloud Forest? +
The supplied pricing table reports ₹2.20–2.33 Cr for 2 BHK, ₹2.82–3.18 Cr for 3 BHK and ₹3.49 Cr for 3.5 BHK + Home Office.
What configurations are available? +
The primary pricing table covers 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 3.5 BHK + Home Office. Detailed material also references 4 BHK homes of approximately 2,500–2,600 sq.ft., but pricing is not supplied.
What is the base price? +
₹15,690/sq.ft.
What is the possession date? +
The supplied research states RERA completion as 1 October 2029. Verify the current RERA record before publishing.
How far is the metro? +
Whitefield/Kadugodi Metro is reported at approximately 2.5 km.
How far is Hope Farm? +
Hope Farm Circle is reported at approximately 1.2 km.
Is it a low-density project? +
No. The supplied assessment calculates approximately 89 homes/acre, using 1,330 homes across approximately 15 acres.
What are the biggest risks? +
Peak-hour Hope Farm/Whitefield congestion, phase density, shared infrastructure dependency, 2029 delivery and a narrower residential execution track record are the key concerns.
Who is it best suited for? +
ITPL/Whitefield professionals, families wanting metro/employment proximity, and buyers comfortable paying a premium for an integrated-city and nature-led proposition.
