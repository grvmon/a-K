How to Calculate Your Interior Budget

Planning a complete home interior budget involves evaluating base woodwork rates, allocating funds for key functional zones like the kitchen and wardrobes, and maintaining an emergency contingency reserve.

Step 1
Calculate Base Budget
Multiply Property Built-up Area (Sq. Ft.) by your baseline interior rate per sq. ft.
Step 2
Add Component Scope
Add separate line items for modular kitchen, wardrobes, loose furniture, lighting, false ceiling, and appliances.
Step 3
Add Contingency Reserve
Apply a 10% contingency buffer to account for unforeseen site modifications and design additions.
Step 4
Track Actual Spend
Track vendor invoices against your planned budget to maintain complete financial control.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does 3BHK interior cost per sq. ft. in India?

Home interior design in India generally ranges from ₹1,200 to ₹2,500 per sq. ft. for standard to premium finishes, including modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceiling, and lighting.

How do I prevent double-counting in my interior quote?

Always verify whether your contractor's per sq. ft. quote includes the modular kitchen or appliances. If included, do not add separate line items for those categories.

How much contingency buffer should I maintain for home interiors?

Maintaining a 10% contingency buffer is recommended to account for design revisions, electrical point modifications, transportation and unexpected civil adjustments.
