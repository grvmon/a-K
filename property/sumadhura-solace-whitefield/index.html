About Sumadhura Solace

Sumadhura Solace (Codename E-119) is a premium 11.66-acre residential development situated on Marathahalli Main Road, Thubarahalli, Whitefield. Designed for discerning homebuyers, the project features 7 architecturally refined towers (2B + G + 18 floors) surrounded by serene twin lakes.

With an exceptional 81% open space ratio, the community offers spacious 3 & 4 BHK residences, a 45,000 sq.ft. luxury clubhouse, and 90+ amenities. Its strategic positioning balances high-rise architectural elegance with peaceful lakefront living.

Things to Know Before Booking

  • Construction Timeline: Target possession is December 2029 (under-construction holding period).
  • Pricing Premium: Lake-facing units carry a price premium over internal podium-facing units.
  • Feeder Road Traffic: Peak-hour traffic on Marathahalli Main Road feeder routes requires commute planning.
  • Floor-Plan Loading: SBUA-to-carpet loading factor averages ~35% across configurations.
  • Document Verification: Title clearance and approved plan sanction copies should be verified prior to booking.
