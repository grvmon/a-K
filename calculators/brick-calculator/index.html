How to Calculate Bricks Required

Estimating bricks for a wall requires calculating net wall volume, determining the effective unit volume of a single brick including mortar joint, and applying a breakage wastage allowance.

Step 1
Gross Wall Volume
Multiply Length × Height × Thickness to get the total gross 3D wall volume.
Step 2
Deduct Openings
Subtract the combined 3D volume of doors and windows (Opening Area × Wall Thickness).
Step 3
Brick Module Volume
Add mortar joint thickness (10 mm) to length, width, and height of the brick: (L+J) × (W+J) × (H+J).
Step 4
Divide & Add Wastage
Divide Net Wall Volume by Brick Module Volume, then add 5% to 10% wastage for site breakage.

How Is Mortar Estimated?

The calculator estimates theoretical mortar volume by comparing total net wall volume with the volume occupied by dry bricks:

Mortar Volume = Net Wall Volume − (Bricks Required × Actual Brick Volume without Mortar)

Mortar typically accounts for 20% to 25% of the total brickwork volume in standard 9-inch brick walls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bricks are needed for a 9-inch wall of 100 sq. ft.?

For a 9-inch thick wall of 100 sq. ft. using standard 9x4.5x3 in red bricks and 10mm mortar joint (with 5% wastage), you need approximately 1,148 bricks.

How is mortar volume calculated in brickwork?

Mortar volume is calculated by subtracting the actual volume of dry bricks from the net wall volume.

What is the standard size of a red brick in India?

The standard nominal size of a modular red brick in India is 9 × 4.5 × 3 inches (or 225 × 112.5 × 75 mm).
