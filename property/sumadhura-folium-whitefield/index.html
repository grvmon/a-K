About Sumadhura Folium

Sumadhura Folium is a 16.5-acre residential community located on Borewell Road in Core Whitefield, positioned for family-oriented buyers seeking expansive open spaces alongside quick connectivity to East Bengaluru's primary employment hubs.

The development comprises 7 towers (2B + G + 19 floors) housing approximately ~1,300 residences across 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Study, 4 BHK, and 4 BHK + Study configurations ranging from 1,515 to 2,435 sq.ft.

Acre&Key's assessment highlights the project's balanced core proposition: prime Core Whitefield location (~3 km to ITPL/EPIP and ~2–3 km to Purple Line Metro) paired with an 83% reported open-space ratio and a ~54,000 sq.ft. multi-level clubhouse with 120+ amenities.

Important verification points for buyers include peak-hour traffic bottlenecks along Borewell Road and a possession date reconciliation point between Phase IV RERA filings (31 March 2028) and builder marketing materials (December 2027).

INDEPENDENT ADVISORY VERDICT

Acre&Key Verdict

Core Whitefield + Metro-led Family Living

Sumadhura Folium scores 3.9 / 5 in Acre&Key's independent project assessment.

The project presents a balanced Core Whitefield proposition with strong metro (~2–3 km) and ITPL access (~3 km), established social infrastructure, and a meaningful amenity ecosystem (~54,000 sq.ft. clubhouse & 83% open space).

The primary appeal centers on family-oriented 3 & 4 BHK layouts within a mature residential corridor. However, buyers should remain mindful of peak-hour Borewell Road congestion, project density (~1,300 units), and phase-specific possession timeline reconciliation.
