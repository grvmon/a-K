About Sumadhura Solace

Sumadhura Solace is a premium residential project located in the heart of Whitefield, Bengaluru. Spread across 11.66 acres with 81% open space, it offers spacious 3 & 4 BHK apartments with twin lake views, a 45,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, and 90+ world-class amenities.

Designed for discerning homebuyers seeking tranquil living amidst urban convenience, Sumadhura Solace balances high-rise architectural elegance with expansive green landscapes and dedicated wellness zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sumadhura Solace RERA approved?
Yes, Sumadhura Solace is fully RERA approved under Karnataka RERA registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/111225/008330.
Where is Sumadhura Solace located?
Sumadhura Solace is located in Thubarahalli, Whitefield, Bengaluru, offering direct connectivity to ITPL, Outer Ring Road, and Whitefield Metro station.
What is the possession date for Sumadhura Solace?
The target possession date for Sumadhura Solace Phase 1 is December 2029.
What configurations and unit sizes are offered?
The project offers 3 BHK (1,865 – 2,050 sq.ft.) and 4 BHK (2,450 – 2,800 sq.ft.) luxury residences.
