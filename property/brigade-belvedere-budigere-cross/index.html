About Brigade Belvedere

Brigade Belvedere is positioned as a value-oriented branded residential community in Budigere Cross. Its proposition combines Brigade branding, direct regional-road access and a broad entry-to-family configuration ladder.

The supplied research describes an Italian-inspired aesthetic identity and a privacy-led "bungalow in the sky" positioning, with landscaped and amenity-led elements across the masterplan.

The project is most relevant for buyers who are comfortable trading mature Core Whitefield convenience for a lower entry point and a longer-term Budigere growth story.

Diligence Note: Tower and home counts vary between the primary pricing table and detailed scheme assessment (10.7 acres, 5 towers, 1,750 homes). Verify applicable phase specifics before booking.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Brigade Belvedere FAQs

Essential answers for prospective buyers
What is the price of Brigade Belvedere? +
The supplied primary pricing table reports approximately ₹0.93–1.05 Cr for 1 BHK + 1T, ₹1.41–1.62 Cr for 2 BHK + 2T, ₹1.72–2.02 Cr for 3 BHK + 2T, ₹2.15–2.46 Cr for 3 BHK + 3T and ₹2.51–2.79 Cr for 3 BHK + Study + Powder.
What configurations are available? +
1 BHK + 1T, 2 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T and 3 BHK + Study + Powder.
What are the sizes? +
1 BHK + 1T: 715–732 sq.ft. · 2 BHK + 2T: 1,111–1,149 sq.ft. · 3 BHK + 2T: 1,365–1,443 sq.ft. · 3 BHK + 3T: 1,720–1,767 sq.ft. · 3 BHK + Study + Powder: 2,013 sq.ft.
What is the base price? +
The supplied scheme reports a base price of ₹11,000/sq.ft.
What are the additional charges? +
PLC up to ₹500/sq.ft., floor rise ₹25/sq.ft. per floor, clubhouse ₹4.5 lakh, power/infrastructure ₹425/sq.ft., maintenance ₹72/sq.ft. and deposit/corpus ₹72/sq.ft.
Is Brigade Belvedere close to the metro? +
No. The supplied research reports Whitefield/Kadugodi Metro at approximately 9.7–12 km depending on route.
How far is ITPL? +
The detailed assessment reports ITPL at approximately 12 km.
What is the possession date? +
The supplied research states RERA completion as 31 March 2031. Verify the applicable phase's current RERA record before publishing a definitive date.
How large is the project? +
The detailed assessment describes approximately 10.7 acres, 5 towers and 1,750 homes, while the primary pricing table contains different tower-level information. Verify the applicable phase before booking.
What is the project density? +
The detailed assessment calculates approximately 163 homes per acre, using 1,750 homes across 10.75 acres.
Is it good for end use? +
It is best suited to value-seeking buyers who are comfortable with Budigere's developing ecosystem and a longer commute trade-off in exchange for a branded project and lower entry price.
Is it good for investment? +
It can suit a longer-horizon value thesis, but current locality maturity, competing supply, high density and delayed rental potential make it less suitable as a near-term rental play.
What are the biggest risks? +
Very high density, developing social infrastructure, limited metro access, Budigere junction/last-mile congestion, long delivery horizon and competing supply.
