How is Home Interior Cost Calculated?

The quoted interior rate per sq. ft. rarely represents your final interior budget. Component upgrades, modular kitchen fittings, wardrobes, false ceiling, furniture, and contingency buffers materially increase the effective cost per sq. ft.

Step 1
Base Interior Work
Property Size × Base Quoted Interior Rate per Sq. Ft.
Step 2
Fixed Component Upgrades
Modular kitchen, wardrobes, electrical fittings, false ceiling, civil work, and loose furniture.
Step 3
Contingency Buffer
A 10% to 15% safety buffer on the subtotal for unexpected design modifications or site variations.
Step 4
Effective Cost per Sq. Ft.
Total Interior Budget divided by total property sq. ft. area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is effective cost per sq ft higher than quoted base rate?

Quoted base rates per sq ft typically cover basic woodwork and laminates. Adding modular kitchens, wardrobes, electrical work, false ceiling, furniture, and a contingency buffer increases your effective cost per sq ft.

What contingency percentage should I budget for home interiors?

A 10% to 15% contingency buffer is recommended to account for design modifications, site variations, material upgrades, and unforeseen structural or electrical adjustments during execution.

How much does a modular kitchen usually cost?

A standard modular kitchen for a 2BHK or 3BHK home typically ranges between ₹2.5 Lakhs and ₹6 Lakhs depending on carcass material (HDF/PLY), finish (acrylic, laminate, PU), hardware fittings, and appliances.

Is false ceiling included in base interior packages?

Not usually. Most basic interior contractors quote false ceiling separately on a per-square-foot basis of ceiling area, ranging between ₹90 and ₹150 per sq ft.
