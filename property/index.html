Frequently Asked Questions

Institutional answers to common questions about buying residential property in Bengaluru.

How does acre&key evaluate residential properties in Bengaluru?
acre&key applies an institutional due diligence methodology examining land title integrity, RERA sanction compliance, structural floor plan efficiency (carpet-to-super-built-up ratio), builder track record, and micro-market pricing fundamentals before issuing an evidence-based assessment.
Are all residential projects on acre&key RERA registered?
Yes. Every featured residential project in Bengaluru is verified against the Karnataka RERA (K-RERA) portal for sanctioned building plans, commencement certificates, encumbrance certificates, and completion deadlines.
Does acre&key charge home buyers for property evaluation and advisory?
Our primary advisory call, property shortlisting, and comparative asset dossier evaluations are completely free for home buyers. We provide structured, buyer-first guidance across Tier-1 builder developments in East Bengaluru.
Which micro-markets in Bengaluru are covered?
Our current coverage focuses on key high-growth micro-markets in East Bengaluru including Whitefield, Budigere Cross, Budigere Road, Varthur, and Greater Whitefield (Old Madras Road / Hoskote corridor).
