How do you use the Section 80C tax savings calculator?

  1. Enter your taxable income before 80C: salary after the ₹ 50,000 standard deduction and any other deductions, plus other income.
  2. Enter what you paid this year towards home loan principal, EPF, PPF and ELSS. Life insurance, tuition fees, stamp duty and other items are under “Add insurance, tuition, stamp duty and others”.
  3. Read the tax saved, how much of the ₹ 1.5 lakh limit you have used, and what a top-up would save.

What is Section 80C under the new Income-tax Act?

From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act. The old sections 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD(1) are now section 123, with the eligible items listed in Schedule XV. The combined limit stays at ₹ 1.5 lakh a year, and the deduction is available only in the old tax regime. The new regime, which is the default, does not allow it.

For home buyers, two items matter. Principal repaid on a home loan from a lender such as a bank, housing finance company or employer counts, and so do stamp duty, registration fee and transfer charges in the year you pay them. If you sell the home within five years of the end of the year you got possession, the deduction already claimed is added back to your income in the year of sale.

Worked example: ₹ 12 lakh taxable income

₹ 50,000 of home loan principal, ₹ 40,000 of EPF, ₹ 20,000 of PPF, ₹ 30,000 of ELSS, ₹ 20,000 of life insurance and ₹ 10,000 of tuition fees add up to ₹ 1,70,000. Only ₹ 1.5 lakh counts. Tax falls from ₹ 1,79,400 to ₹ 1,32,600, a saving of ₹ 46,800, because the whole deduction comes off income taxed at 30%. The extra ₹ 20,000 saves nothing.

Worked example: ₹ 8 lakh taxable income, room left

With only EPF of ₹ 40,000 and life insurance of ₹ 20,000, the deduction is ₹ 60,000 and the saving is ₹ 12,480. The remaining ₹ 90,000 of the limit, if filled, would save about ₹ 18,720 more at the 20% slab.

What should you keep in mind?

  • The deduction lowers your tax only if you file under the old regime. Check both regimes with the home loan tax benefit calculator.
  • Home loan interest is a separate deduction (section 22, the old section 24(b)) and does not use up the ₹ 1.5 lakh limit.
  • Only payments made during the year count, and investments such as PPF and ELSS come with lock-in periods.
