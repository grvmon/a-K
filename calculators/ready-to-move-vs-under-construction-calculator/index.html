How do you use this calculator?

  1. Enter the price of the ready flat and of the under-construction flat you are comparing.
  2. Enter the months until possession, including the delay you think is likely, and the rent you would pay meanwhile.
  3. Open “Adjust rates, loan and GST” to change the down payment (25%, the same for both), the GST on the new flat, the loan rate (8.5%), the tenure, whether the ready flat is resale or new, and the stamp duty rates.

Ready-to-move or under-construction: what are you comparing?

An under-construction flat is usually priced lower, but you pay for the wait. The calculator adds 5% GST to it and none to the ready flat. You pay interest on the loan as the bank releases it to the builder, before your EMIs start. And you keep paying rent until you get the keys.

With a ready flat you move in at once and start the full EMI. The calculator adds up what each choice costs from today until the day you get the keys, and leaves out loan principal, which builds your equity rather than costing you.

Worked example: ₹ 1 Cr ready flat or ₹ 90 L new flat, 24 months away

The ready resale flat costs ₹ 1,00,00,000 plus ₹ 7,60,000 in stamp duty and registration. With a ₹ 75 L loan at 8.5%, the interest part of the first 24 EMIs is ₹ 12,50,354. Cost to the keys: ₹ 1,20,10,354.

The new flat costs ₹ 90,00,000 plus 5% GST (₹ 4,50,000) and ₹ 6,84,000 in stamp duty and registration. Pre-EMI interest on a ₹ 67.5 L loan drawn over 24 months is ₹ 5,97,656, and 24 months of rent at ₹ 30,000 is ₹ 7,20,000. Cost to the keys: ₹ 1,14,51,656, which is ₹ 5,58,697 less than the ready flat.

What the numbers do not show

  • Delivery risk. Check the project’s RERA registration and the builder’s record. Have a lawyer review the agreement.
  • Price growth while you wait. The calculator does not assume any.
  • The age and condition of a resale flat, and its remaining life.
  • Tax treatment of pre-EMI interest. Ask your CA.

Which buying costs apply in Bengaluru?

On a sale deed in Bengaluru, Karnataka charges 5% stamp duty on the market value (Karnataka Stamp Act, Schedule Art. 20(1)), a 10% cess on the duty (s.3B) and, inside the Greater Bengaluru Area, a 2% surcharge on the duty (Greater Bengaluru Governance Act 2024, s.170). The registration fee is 2% (IGR notification of 29 Aug 2025, in force from 31 Aug 2025). Together that is 7.6% of the value. The first sale of a flat pays less duty: 2% up to ₹ 20 L and 3% above ₹ 20 L up to ₹ 45 L, so the total is 4.24% or 5.36%. Resale flats, plots and houses pay 5% at any price. Rates verified 18 Oct 2026 · confirm on Kaveri Online before registering.

GST on an under-construction home is 5%, or 1% for an affordable home, per the CBIC real estate FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

Ready-to-move is not always cheaper. An under-construction flat often has a lower price, but you add 5% GST, pre-EMI interest and rent until possession. For a ₹ 1 crore ready flat against a ₹ 90 lakh new flat 24 months away, the new flat costs ₹ 5,58,697 less to the keys at ₹ 30,000 rent.

GST is charged on under-construction flats at 5%, or 1% for affordable homes, per the CBIC real estate FAQ. This calculator adds no GST to the ready flat; whether GST applies to a particular ready purchase depends on the facts of the sale. Indicative only. Please verify with your Chartered Accountant before acting.

Pre-EMI interest is the interest you pay on the part of the home loan the bank has released to the builder, before construction ends and full EMIs begin. It does not reduce the loan. The calculator assumes the loan is released evenly up to possession.

Each month of delay costs the rent you keep paying plus a month of interest on the loan already released. With ₹ 30,000 rent and a ₹ 67.5 lakh loan at 8.5%, that is about ₹ 77,812 a month once most of the loan is out.

Stamp duty is lower on the first sale of a flat up to ₹ 45 lakh: 2% up to ₹ 20 lakh and 3% above that, against 5% for resale. Above ₹ 45 lakh both pay 5%. With cess, surcharge and the 2% registration fee, the totals are 4.24%, 5.36% and 7.6%.

Before buying an under-construction flat, check the project’s RERA registration and its approved completion date, the builder’s past deliveries, and the payment schedule in the agreement. Have a lawyer review the agreement for sale and the title before you sign.
