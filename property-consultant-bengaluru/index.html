How is buying with a consultant different?

Most buyers start with a list of projects. We start with your budget, where you work, who lives with you, and when you need to move.

The list then gets short. For each home we look at the layout, the location, the developer's record and the risks, and tell you what we found.

What real estate consultancy services do you offer?

Property shortlisting

Homes matched to your budget, commute and household. You spend site visits on a few serious options.

Project and developer checks

Layouts, specifications, amenities, Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority (K-RERA) registration and the developer's delivery record, set side by side. The builder trust score is a useful place to start.

Due diligence

A structured evaluation of what checks out, what is unclear, and which documents your lawyer should verify. You can check a property's risk score before a visit.

General information, not legal advice. Please consult a property lawyer before you sign.

Price, total cost and negotiation

The quoted price is rarely what you pay. We add stamp duty, registration, GST and other charges, compare similar homes, and negotiate price and terms where there is room.

Use the total cost of buying a home calculator, including stamp duty and registration charges in Karnataka. If you may rent the home out, the rental yield calculator shows the arithmetic. Rent and price growth are not assured.

Frequently asked questions about property consultants in Bengaluru

What does a real estate consultant do?

A real estate consultant helps you choose a property and buy it on fair terms. That usually means shortlisting homes, checking projects and developers, working out the total cost, flagging risks and negotiating. What's included varies, so ask any consultant for their exact scope before you start.

How do I choose a property consultant in Bengaluru?

Choose a property consultant in Bengaluru who knows the areas you're considering, explains how they assess a property, and tells you clearly how they get paid. Ask to see a sample evaluation and a clear account of how they are paid.

What are real estate consultancy services?

Real estate consultancy services are the help a consultant gives a buyer before purchase: shortlisting, project and developer checks, due diligence support, cost and price analysis, and negotiation. Legal title checks should still be done by a qualified property lawyer.

How do I find a real estate consultant near me?

To find a real estate consultant near you, search for consultants who actively work in the areas where you want to buy, then compare their services, experience and fee disclosures. acre&key works across east and south-east Bengaluru, including Whitefield, Sarjapur Road and Bellandur.

What is the difference between a real estate consultant and a property broker?

A broker mainly helps a deal happen, while a consultant spends more time helping you decide which property to buy and whether the terms are fair. The roles often overlap, so ask exactly what is included and how the consultant is paid.

Does acre&key charge buyers an advisory fee?

No. acre&key doesn't charge homebuyers an advisory fee. Any commercial arrangements are disclosed on request.

Can a real estate investment consultant estimate my returns?

A real estate investment consultant can estimate likely rent, rental yield, total costs, competing supply and resale demand, and show you a downside case. These are estimates, not promises. Actual returns depend on the market and the property, and tax points should be checked with a Chartered Accountant.
