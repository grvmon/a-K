02. Verbal Identity & Lexicon

HNI buyers ignore generic marketing jargon. We are buyer-first, data-driven advisors. Never use terms that imply urgency or generic "luxury".

Voice & Tone
✕ BAD

"Don't miss out on this breathtaking, ultra-luxury masterpiece in Whitefield! Perfect for your dream lifestyle!"

Why it fails: Sounds like every generic broker. Reeks of desperation.

✓ GOOD

"A Grade-A asset in the Whitefield micro-market. Strong capital appreciation fundamentals supported by Phase 2 metro connectivity."

Why it works: Specific, sourced, calm.

Mechanics
Currency & Pricing

Rule: Always use ₹ with spaces. Use 'Cr' and 'L'. Always include decimals.

3.1Cr, Rs 3.10 Cr, 3,10,00,000

₹ 3.10 Cr

Area & Measurements

Rule: Title Case with periods. Commas for >999.

3150 sqft, 3,150 Sqft

3,150 Sq.Ft.

Headlines & Body

Rule: Strictly Sentence case. Never exclamation marks.

Secure Your Asset Today!

Schedule a clinical analysis.

06. Layout & Spacing

Fibonacci grids and 1280px max-width container structures.

Spatial & Layout Variables

  • Fibonacci Scale: Margins and paddings follow a Fibonacci rhythm (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8).
  • Section Spacing: Desktop: 110px | Mobile: 60px
  • Container Max Width: 1280px (Aligning to Rule of Thirds grid points)
  • Blur Scale: Nav/Glassmorphism uses 16px blur. Modals use 8px blur.
  • Z-Index: Modals (1000) > Header (900) > Dropdowns (800) > Tooltips (700) > Base Content (1)

Section Theming (Alternating Backgrounds)

To create visual pacing on long-scroll pages without relying on harsh drop shadows, we use Banded Sections. The page background must strictly alternate between these three environments to delineate different content blocks.

Base Canvas

Titanium Frost

The default background for the majority of the page. Creates a soft, premium foundation.

Alternating Canvas

Pure White

Used for the very next section to create subtle, shadow-free separation from the Frost canvas above it.

High-Impact Breakout

Obsidian Black

Used sparingly to break the flow. Reserved for footers, lead-generation forms, and massive value propositions.

11. Actions & Inputs

HNI clients do not fill out "squeeze pages." Our forms must feel like secure, private banking applications. No bubbly inputs. We use strict 0.5px borders, 4px radiuses, and crisp typography.

Clinical Badges & Tags (The Anti-Pill Rule)

Standard web design uses rounded "pill" tags (border-radius: 4px). We strictly reject this. All tags (RERA, Status, Scores) must use our rigid 4px geometry to maintain an architectural, blueprint-like aesthetic.

Under Construction RERA Verified Score: [X.XX]/5

Cinematic Modals & Overlays

Pop-ups should not feel like marketing interruptions. Modal backdrops must use a heavy cinematic blur (backdrop-filter: blur(8px)) with a deep, desaturated overlay (rgba(10,10,10,0.85)) to completely focus the user's attention.

Floorplan Access

Text-on-Image Legibility

Placing raw text directly over a photograph severely compromises legibility. We enforce a strict "Anchored Gradient" rule. Whenever text sits on an image, an Obsidian Black gradient (#2B2D31, fading from 95% opacity to 0% at the 60% mark) must anchor the text from the bottom or top, targeting a 7.0:1 AAA contrast ratio.

Protected Legibility

Text is anchored by the Obsidian Black gradient, ignoring the chaotic image behind it.

The "Editorial Card" Standard

Data cards should never use default CSS box-shadows. Shadows look like generic web templates. Our cards use strict 0.5px hairlines, generous internal padding (min 1.5rem), and rely entirely on typography sizing to establish hierarchy.

09. Graphic Language

The visual grammar of the brand. How compositional elements create hierarchy without decoration.

The 70/30 Rule

Light dominates.

70% light canvas, 30% structured content. Never fill the page. Whitespace is the loudest design element.

Hairline Architecture

0.5px borders replace shadows.

Hairlines are our structural dividers. They mimic high-end financial print and enforce hierarchy without visual weight.

Fibonacci-derived spacing.

24. Accessibility

  • Focus & Keyboard: All interactive elements must support standard :focus-visible (2px ring in var(--text-copper-aaa) on light backgrounds or var(--highlight-peach) on dark backgrounds). Tab indexing must flow logically from top-left to bottom-right.
  • ARIA Patterns: Accordions must toggle aria-expanded. Tabs require aria-selected. Modals mandate a focus-trap and must respond to the Esc key to close.
  • Touch Equivalents: Hover-only tooltips or card-dimming effects must have a persistent or tap-equivalent display on mobile.
Granular Contrast Requirements by Component Type
1. Text & Headings (WCAG 1.4.3 AAA)

Strict 7.0:1 minimum for body copy and metadata. Minimum 4.5:1 for large display headings (≥18pt or ≥14pt bold).

2. Buttons & Inputs (WCAG 1.4.11 AA)

Interactive control perimeters, input borders, and primary CTA buttons must achieve ≥3.0:1 boundary definition against adjacent surfaces.

3. Gradients & Photographic Overlays

Direct text on photographs is banned. The 95% Obsidian Black anchored gradient provides a solid dark base yielding ≥19.8:1 AAA contrast on white text.

4. Hover & Focus States

Focus rings mandate 2px solid outlines (≥3.0:1). Cinematic image dimming (0.4s ease) must never degrade overlying typography below AAA thresholds.

5. Disabled States (WCAG 1.4.3 Exemption)

Inactive elements are exempt from minimum contrast ratios; styled consistently with opacity: 0.45, cursor: not-allowed, and pointer-events: none.
