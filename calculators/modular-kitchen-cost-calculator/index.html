How to Calculate Modular Kitchen Cost

Calculating the true budget for a modular kitchen requires evaluating cabinet structure, shutter finishes, countertop slabs, hardware accessories, labour charges, and appliances.

Step 1
Calculate Cabinet Cost
Multiply Total Running Feet by Cabinet Rate per running ft (Plywood / HDHMR).
Step 2
Add Shutter & Countertop
Add separate finish charges (if applicable) and countertop slab material cost (Sq. Ft. × Rate).
Step 3
Add Hardware & Appliances
Include soft-close tandem drawers, pantry pull-outs, hob, chimney, oven, and installation labour.
Step 4
Add Contingency & Taxes
Apply 5% contingency buffer and applicable GST to derive the final all-in budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an L-shaped modular kitchen cost in India?

An L-shaped modular kitchen (10-14 running ft) in India typically costs ₹1.8 Lakhs to ₹3.5 Lakhs for marine plywood cabinetry, laminate/acrylic finishes, granite countertop and basic soft-close hardware.

What is included in kitchen running foot pricing?

Running foot pricing usually includes base and wall cabinet carcasses and shutters. Countertop, tandem drawers, appliances, and GST are frequently billed separately.

Which material is best for modular kitchen cabinets?

BWP Grade (710) Marine Plywood is the most durable choice for Indian kitchens due to superior moisture resistance, structural strength and screw retention.
