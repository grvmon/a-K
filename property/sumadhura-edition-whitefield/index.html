About Sumadhura Edition

Sumadhura Edition is positioned as a premium large-format residential community in Siddapura / Core Whitefield, combining established employment access, social infrastructure and a broad 2–4 BHK product mix.

The project is designed for buyers seeking an upscale Whitefield address within an established residential and employment ecosystem.

Its proposition is driven not only by the homes themselves, but by the depth of the surrounding Core Whitefield ecosystem.

The key diligence consideration is scope: the research describes a roughly 20-acre, 11-tower, approximately 1,650-home masterplan across two phases, while the registered phase needs to be reconciled against the aggregate masterplan.
