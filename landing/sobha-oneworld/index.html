About One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld

One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld is positioned as part of a 48-acre integrated township in the OMR / Greater Whitefield corridor. The masterplan combines 1–4 BHK residences with large-scale amenities, landscaped spaces and a wider community ecosystem.

The proposition is less about immediate convenience and more about township scale, SOBHA's integrated construction platform, broad home choices and long-term community development. For buyers, the trade-off is a long delivery horizon, multiple RERA phases and a surrounding ecosystem that is still developing.

Key Highlights
•Integrated Township: 48-acre masterplan with residential and wider township infrastructure.
•Broad Configuration Range: 1–4 BHK residences from 734–2,415 sq.ft.
•SOBHA Construction Platform: Integrated construction capability and established developer track record.
•Landscape-Led Masterplan: Five themed landscape zones and ~100,000 sq.ft. reported clubhouse / amenity offering.
Due Diligence
•Long Delivery Horizon: Six RERA phases extend the completion horizon into 2032–2033.
•Phase Dependency: The overall township will develop progressively across multiple phases.
•Developing Catchment: The surrounding social and rental ecosystem is less mature than established Whitefield.
•Metro & Commute: Current connectivity is road-led, with the proposed Purple Line station reported at ~4.3 km.

LOCATION & CONNECTIVITY

OMR / Greater Whitefield • A Future-Growth Corridor

A road-led location positioned around Greater Whitefield, OMR and the Hoskote growth corridor.

One Residences is located along OMR in Greater Whitefield, near Hoskote. The location proposition is primarily a long-term growth and township-scale thesis rather than an immediate walkability or metro-convenience play.

ITPL is approximately 25 minutes away in off-peak conditions, while the proposed Purple Line is approximately 4.3 km away. For daily commuting, road connectivity remains the primary consideration.
