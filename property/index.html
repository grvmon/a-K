Frequently Asked Questions

Evidence-based answers to key questions about evaluating and buying residential property in Bengaluru.

How do I safely evaluate and buy residential property in Bengaluru?
Buying residential property in Bengaluru requires a structured evaluation across financial feasibility, legal sanctions, and builder execution credibility. Start by defining your micro-market parameters based on daily commute corridors such as the Outer Ring Road, Whitefield IT corridor, or Bengaluru Airport expressway. Verify that the project holds an active Karnataka RERA (K-RERA) registration with approved building plans from local planning authorities (BDA, BBMP, BMRDA, or BIAPPA). Conduct a comprehensive 30-year title search through a qualified property lawyer to verify unencumbered ownership before signing an agreement of sale or remitting booking deposits.
What mandatory legal documents must be verified before purchasing real estate in Bengaluru?
Essential due diligence documents for purchasing property in Bengaluru include: 1) Mother Deed and chain of title documents tracing clear ownership for at least 30 years; 2) Karnataka RERA Registration Certificate and sanctioned layout plan; 3) BBMP or BDA A-Khata certificate and latest Khata extract; 4) Commencement Certificate (CC) confirming structural construction follows sanctioned limits; 5) Nil Encumbrance Certificate (EC) in Form 15 covering the past 15 to 30 years from Kaveri Online Services; 6) Property tax paid receipts; and 7) Occupancy Certificate (OC) issued by the local planning authority if purchasing a ready-to-move apartment.
What are the current stamp duty and registration charges for property in Bengaluru?
In Karnataka, stamp duty and registration fees on residential property sales are calculated on the higher of the agreement consideration value or the official Kaveri guidance value. The applicable costs include: 1) Stamp Duty of 5% on property values above ₹45 Lakhs; 2) Surcharge of 2% within BBMP municipal limits (or 3% in rural/BMRDA areas) on the basic stamp duty; 3) 10% Cess on Stamp Duty; and 4) Registration Fee of 1% (recently rationalized under Karnataka Stamp Act amendments). Buyers should also account for advocate legal inspection fees, bank processing charges, and applicable 5% GST on under-construction apartments.
Which are the top residential micro-markets in Bengaluru for end-use and capital growth?
The leading residential micro-markets in Bengaluru are anchored by major tech employment hubs and high-speed infrastructure: 1) Whitefield and Hope Farm: Premier East Bengaluru corridor with operational Namma Metro Purple Line, established healthcare, and top international schools; 2) Budigere Cross and Budigere Road: High-growth northern extension of East Bengaluru offering institutional-grade integrated townships near Old Madras Road; 3) Sarjapur Road and Bellandur: Strategic corridor connecting the Outer Ring Road (ORR) tech belt with southeastern commercial clusters; and 4) Devanahalli and Airport Road: Fast-evolving North Bengaluru growth node propelled by KIADB Aerospace Park, Hardware Tech Park, and Namma Metro Blue Line expansion.

Are under-construction residential properties safe to buy in Bengaluru?
Under-construction properties in Bengaluru are substantially safer following the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA). K-RERA mandates that developers deposit 70% of buyer collections into a dedicated escrow account utilized exclusively for construction and land expenses. However, buyers must review K-RERA quarterly progress reports (QPR), verify that construction timelines align with RERA delivery commitments, confirm the existence of a valid Commencement Certificate (CC), and evaluate the developer's historical delivery track record across past phases before committing funds.
