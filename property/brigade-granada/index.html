About Brigade Granada

Brigade Granada is a highly anticipated pre-launch residential township development by the Brigade Group, situated on the prominent Whitefield-Kadugodi / Hoskote Road corridor. Spanning approximately 30 acres, the project is designed as an integrated live-work-play ecosystem featuring 14 high-rise towers (G+24 to 55 floors) and ~2,000 luxury apartments.

The development offers 2.5, 3, and 4 BHK configurations. The architectural vision heavily prioritizes green living, with up to 80% of the land area reportedly dedicated to open green spaces, curated parks, and recreational zones.

The core proposition of Brigade Granada lies in its immense township scale and the Brigade brand’s reliable execution track record. Purchasing during the Expression of Interest (EOI) pre-launch phase typically offers early-mover price advantages before RERA launch and subsequent price hikes.

!Premium FAR Diligence
Please note that projects dependent on "Premium FAR" for elevated floor counts are currently under review by the Supreme Court. While Brigade is a Tier-1 developer, buyers should remain aware that this could impact final floor approvals or timelines.

The masterplan reserves nearly 80% of its footprint for landscaping and open areas, a rarity in this dense corridor. The clubhouse and sports arenas are expected to be built to signature Brigade standards, offering extensive athletic facilities, co-working spaces, and wellness centers.

Configurations begin with highly efficient 2.5 BHK units ideal for small families or hybrid workers, moving up to spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK layouts. Specific dimensional analyses will be published once the RERA-sanctioned floor plans are officially released.

The Whitefield-Kadugodi and Hoskote road micro-market has witnessed steady capital appreciation driven by IT sector expansion and the completion of the Purple Line Metro extension. Brigade's townships historically anchor micro-market pricing, often seeing robust secondary market demand upon completion.

Situated strategically on the Whitefield-Kadugodi road, the project offers excellent connectivity to major IT parks (ITPL, EPIP Zone) while providing a direct exit route towards Hoskote and the upcoming Chennai Expressway.
