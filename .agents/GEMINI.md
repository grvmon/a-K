# Acre & Key Brand & Design Guidelines

## 1. Master Color Palette (Locked & Permanent)
- **Accent Gold / Refined Brass**: `#B88E52` (`--refined-brass: #B88E52;`, `rgba(184, 142, 82, ...)`)
  - Used for: All primary CTA buttons, timeline step numbers (01–04), active progress traces, key italic accents, badges, and icon highlights.
- **Canvas Base / Warm Linen Ivory**: `#F7F3EC` (`--warm-ivory: #F7F3EC;`)
  - Used for: 75% site background canvas. Provides rich parchment warmth that lets pure white cards stand out.
- **Deep Slate Navy (Primary Ink & Structural Boxes)**: `#1F2B38` / `rgba(31, 43, 56, 0.84)` (`--deep-navy`)
  - Used for: All primary titles, H1–H4 headings, 1-line Summary Banner boxes, mobile drawer headers, and site footer foundation.
- **Card Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White with 8px radius and subtle ambient drop shadow).
- **Supporting Neutrals**:
  - Dark Brass Accent: `#8F662A`
  - Slate Gray (Subtitles/Meta): `#64748B` / `#6B7280`
  - Deep Slate: `#4B5563`
  - Warm Stone (Pill tags): `#EDE8E2`
  - Hairline Dividers & Borders: `#E9E2D7` / `#DDD7CF` / `rgba(184, 142, 82, 0.25)`

## 2. Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `'Marcellus', serif` (Weight 400, editorial architectural luxury)
- **Body & UI Elements**: `'Manrope', sans-serif` (Weights 400, 500, 600, 700)
- **Brand Logo**: `'Josefin Sans', sans-serif` (Weight 600)

## 3. Layout & Geometry
- **Container Widths**: Maximum container width is **1280px** with `2rem` side padding (`1216px` active content area) for a balanced cinematic feel.
- **Editorial Columns**: Reading copy and paragraph text should remain constrained to ~`760px` for optimal readability.
- **Corner Radius**: Unified **`8px`** (`border-radius: 8px`) across all cards, modals, buttons, and badges.
