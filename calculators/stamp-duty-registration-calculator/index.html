How Are Stamp Duty & Registration Charges Calculated?

Stamp Duty is a state-level tax levied on legal property transactions, while Registration Fee is paid to register the property ownership title deed in government records.

Government acquisition charges are calculated using the following steps:

Stamp Duty & Registration Formula Breakdown

1
Step 1 — Calculate Stamp Duty Stamp Duty = Property Value × Stamp Duty Rate%
2
Step 2 — Calculate Registration Fee With Cap: Registration Fee = MIN( Property Value × Registration Rate%, Registration Fee Cap )
Without Cap: Registration Fee = Property Value × Registration Rate%
3
Step 3 — Total Government Charges & Effective Tax Rate Total Government Charges = Stamp Duty + Registration Fee
Effective % = ( Total Government Charges / Property Value ) × 100
4
Step 4 — Total Property Acquisition Cost Total Property Cost = Property Value + Total Government Charges

Key Factors That Influence Stamp Duty Rates in India

Stamp duty rates are non-uniform across India and depend on several key parameters:

  • State Legislation: States like Maharashtra (5%-6%), Karnataka (5%), and Delhi (4%-6%) prescribe distinct rate schedules.
  • Buyer Category (Gender Concessions): Many Indian states offer a 1% to 2% discount on stamp duty when property is registered in a woman's name.
  • Urban vs. Rural Location: Properties within municipal corporation limits often attract an additional 1% local body tax (LBT) or metro cess.
  • Property Type: Commercial real estate transactions generally carry higher stamp duty rates than residential properties.

Stamp Duty & Registration Calculator FAQs

Are stamp duty and registration fees tax-deductible?
Yes. Under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, buyers can claim a tax deduction of up to ₹1.5 Lakhs on stamp duty and registration fees paid during the financial year.
Can stamp duty be included in the home loan amount?
As per RBI guidelines, banks cannot include stamp duty and registration charges in the home loan principal for property values above ₹10 Lakhs. They must be funded separately as cash out-of-pocket.
What happens if a state imposes a maximum registration fee cap?
In states where a cap applies (e.g., ₹30,000 or ₹50,000), the payable registration fee is capped at that amount regardless of how high the property value is.
How is stamp duty calculated for joint property ownership?
If property is jointly registered with a woman as sole or first owner, concessional state rates (typically 1% lower) apply to the total transaction value.
