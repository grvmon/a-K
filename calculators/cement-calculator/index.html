How to Calculate Cement Bags

Calculating cement bags for construction requires determining wet work volume, converting to dry volume, taking the cement fraction from the mix ratio, and applying cement density.

Step 1
Calculate Wet Volume
Multiply Length × Width × Thickness to get wet concrete or mortar volume in cubic feet or cubic meters.
Step 2
Convert to Dry Volume
Multiply wet volume by 1.54 for concrete or 1.33 for mortar to account for void reduction during mixing.
Step 3
Apply Mix Ratio Fraction
For a 1:2:4 mix, cement is 1/(1+2+4) = 1/7 (14.29%) of dry volume. Multiply dry volume by this fraction.
Step 4
Convert Volume to Weight
Multiply cement volume (in m³) by cement density (1440 kg/m³) to determine total net cement weight in kg.

Before Ordering Cement Checklist

  • ✓ Confirm work scope: Concrete, Brickwork, Plaster, or Screed.
  • ✓ Verify approved mix ratio (e.g. M20 1:1.5:3 or M15 1:2:4).
  • ✓ Use correct dry volume factor (1.54 for concrete, 1.33 for mortar).
  • ✓ Include 5% wastage for handling and site spillage.
  • ✓ Store cement bags off the ground in a moisture-free dry shed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bags of cement are needed for 100 cu. ft. of concrete?

For 100 cu. ft. of 1:2:4 nominal concrete (using 1.54 dry volume factor and 5% wastage), you need 19 bags of 50kg cement.

What is the dry volume factor for concrete and mortar?

The dry volume factor is 1.54 for concrete and 1.33 for mortar. It accounts for void reduction when water is added to dry cement, sand, and aggregate.

What is the weight of one standard cement bag in India?

One standard cement bag in India weighs 50 kg and has a volume of approximately 1.226 cu. ft. (0.0347 m³).
