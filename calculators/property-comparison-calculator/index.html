How to Compare Properties Beyond Price

Choosing between two or three properties requires evaluating usable space efficiency, monthly cash flow burden, builder track record, physical layout quality, and legal risk exposure alongside quoted property prices.

Dimension 1
Price per Carpet Sq. Ft.
Evaluates true space efficiency by comparing usable internal carpet square footage against total property cost.
Dimension 2
Monthly Cash Outflow
Combines loan EMI obligations with monthly society maintenance charges to reveal real cash burden.
Dimension 3
Quality & Builder Reliability
Incorporates developer delivery history, RERA compliance, layout usability, and amenities.
Dimension 4
Investment & Legal Risk
Factors in expected appreciation rates, rental yields, and active legal/regulatory risk scores.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Property Comparison Calculator rank properties?

Properties are evaluated on a 100-point weighted score combining Affordability (20%), Investment Potential (20%), Apartment Quality (20%), Location & Connectivity (15%), Builder Trust (10%), Risk-Adjusted Score (10%), and Amenities (5%). The property with the highest score is ranked #1 as your Best Match.

Can I compare 2 properties instead of 3?

Yes. Property C is optional and can be toggled on or off depending on whether you are comparing 2 or 3 real estate options.

Why is price per carpet sq ft more important than total property price?

Comparing price per usable carpet square foot reveals true space efficiency, helping you identify whether an expensive property offers more usable room or simply larger un-usable common areas.

Are the subjective scores independently verified by acreNkey?

No. Subjective ratings (Location, Builder Trust, Quality, Risk) are user-entered screening scores. The calculator provides structured comparison logic based on your inputs.
