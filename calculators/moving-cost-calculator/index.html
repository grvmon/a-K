What Does Moving Cost Include?

Household relocation in India involves multiple cost centers: vehicle transportation fees, specialized multi-layer packing materials (bubble wrap, corrugated boxes), skilled packing labour, loading/unloading teams, stair climbing fees, and transit insurance.

Understanding the Moving Cost Formula

Your total shifting budget is calculated as:

Subtotal = Transport + Packing + Labour + Loading/Unloading + Lift/Floor Fees + Storage

Total Moving Cost = Subtotal + GST Tax (0% - 18%) + Contingency Buffer (5%)

5-Step Moving Day Checklist

Step 01
Declutter Before Packing

Sell or donate unused furniture to reduce overall inventory volume and vehicle size requirement.

Step 02
Obtain 3 Written Quotes

Compare quotes from verified IBA-approved or reputable packers and movers before booking.

Step 03
Verify Service Lift Access

Confirm with society management if service elevators can be reserved during peak shifting hours.

Step 04
Insure Fragile Electronics

Ensure high-value TVs, refrigerators, and artwork are covered under transit insurance policies.

Step 05
Keep Essential Box Separate

Pack first-night essentials (toiletries, chargers, bedsheets, documents) separately in your personal car.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do packers and movers quotes vary so widely?

Moving quotes depend heavily on vehicle capacity, quality of packing materials, number of floor stair flights without service lifts, distance between locations, and peak weekend demand.

Is GST mandatory on packers and movers in India?

GST on goods transport services in India is typically 5% for transport alone or 18% for full-service packing, loading, and transit services provided by registered corporate logistics firms.
