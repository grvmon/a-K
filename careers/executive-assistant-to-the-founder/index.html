1. About Acre&Key

Buying a home is one of the biggest financial decisions most people will ever make. Yet, almost everyone involved in the transaction represents the seller.

At Acre&Key, we're changing that.

We are an independent Home Buyer Concierge that represents only the homebuyer. Through expert advisory, property research, builder evaluation, due diligence, negotiations, and end-to-end buying support, we help our clients make confident, informed decisions.

Our mission is simple: help every homebuyer purchase with clarity and confidence.

2. About the Role

We're looking for an exceptionally organized, proactive, and detail-oriented Executive Assistant to work directly with the Founder.

This is not a traditional administrative role.

You'll become the operational backbone of the Founder's Office, ensuring priorities move forward, meetings translate into action, and critical initiatives never lose momentum.

You'll gain exposure to strategy, operations, marketing, hiring, finance, partnerships, customer experience, and business development while helping build a fast-growing real estate advisory company.

If you enjoy solving problems, bringing order to complexity, and taking ownership, you'll thrive in this role.

3. Key Responsibilities

Executive Support

  • Manage the Founder's calendar, meetings, and daily priorities.
  • Coordinate internal and external meetings.
  • Prepare meeting agendas and briefing notes.
  • Capture meeting minutes and follow up on action items.
  • Handle confidential information with discretion.

Project Management

  • Coordinate strategic initiatives across departments.
  • Maintain project trackers and execution dashboards.
  • Ensure deadlines are met across multiple workstreams.
  • Follow up proactively with internal and external stakeholders.

Operations

  • Improve operational workflows and internal systems.
  • Build and maintain SOPs and documentation.
  • Coordinate with vendors, consultants, and service providers.
  • Assist in hiring, onboarding, and administrative activities.

Research & Business Support

  • Conduct market, competitor, and industry research.
  • Prepare reports, presentations, and business summaries.
  • Gather and analyze information to support decision-making.
  • Organize data into clear, actionable insights.

Communication

  • Draft professional emails, documents, and presentations.
  • Coordinate communication across teams.
  • Ensure smooth information flow between stakeholders.
  • Represent the Founder's Office professionally when interacting with clients and partners.

4. What Success Looks Like

Within your first 90 days, you should be able to:

  • Keep the Founder's calendar running efficiently.
  • Ensure meeting action items are consistently completed.
  • Improve execution across ongoing projects.
  • Reduce operational bottlenecks.
  • Build documentation for recurring processes.
  • Create visibility through structured reports and trackers.
  • Increase the Founder's productivity by eliminating administrative overhead.
