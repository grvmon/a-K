About Sumadhura Folium

A 16.5-acre residential community of ~1,300 homes located on Borewell Road in Core Whitefield.

Planned across 7 residential towers, the development balances contemporary high-rise architecture with expansive open spaces, dedicated lifestyle amenities, and shared residential infrastructure.

The development is planned around:

Residential — multiple apartment configurations and layouts across 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Study, 4 BHK, and 4 BHK + Study formats (1,515–2,435 sq.ft.).
Open Spaces — approximately 83% reported open area across a 16.5-acre parcel.
Amenities — a clubhouse of approximately 54,000 sq.ft.
Masterplan — towers, common spaces, internal movement and shared facilities planned as one residential environment.

The scale of the development makes the masterplan and execution an important part of understanding the project.

acre&key Advisory Verdict

Core Whitefield Scale Meets Mature Corridor · Traffic & 2028 Timeline Deserve Diligence

Sumadhura Folium pairs a prime Borewell Road address and an 83% reported open-space ratio with a ~54,000 sq.ft. multi-level clubhouse. Established social infrastructure and proximity to ITPL/Metro drive strong family appeal, while buyers must factor in peak-hour road bottlenecks and Phase IV March 2028 delivery reconciliation.
