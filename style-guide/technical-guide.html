06 · DESIGN TOKENS & RESPONSIVE SYSTEM

Use these values as the single source of truth. The Fibonacci principle should influence composition and white space—not force every element into a rigid ratio.

Colour rules

  • Canvas: Warm Ivory #F7F3EC
  • Ink: Deep Navy #1F2B38
  • Accent: Antique Brass #B88E52
  • Ratio: 75% light, 20% navy, 5% brass
  • Brass: details, dividers and emphasis only—not body text.

Type hierarchy

  • Display: Marcellus · 40–64px · 1.2
  • H1: Marcellus · 32–48px · 1.2
  • H2: Marcellus · 24–35px · 1.25
  • Body: Manrope · 16px · 1.6
  • Label: Manrope 600 · 12px · uppercase

Space & form

  • Scale: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px
  • Desktop gutter: 24px minimum
  • Section rhythm: 96–120px desktop; 64px mobile
  • Radius: 4px controls; 8px cards
  • Shadows: subtle elevation only; never glossy.

08 · IMAGE & TONE DIRECTION

Use

  • Architectural composition, natural light and tactile materials.
  • Warm, restrained colour grading; real homes and authentic moments.
  • Editorial crops with room for typography and breathing space.
  • Language that sounds like an independent advisor: calm, precise and discreet.

Avoid

  • Generic façades, artificial HDR, stock-smile photography and cluttered collages.
  • Bright gradients, glossy effects, excessive rounded cards or loud promotional badges.
  • Sales-heavy language such as “best deal” or “limited time only”.
  • Using brass for long text, interactive errors or low-contrast labels.
