About Prestige Evergreen

A 24-acre master-planned residential precinct of ~2,000 homes within the 107-acre Prestige Raintree Park integrated township in Varthur.

Planned across 14 residential towers, the development balances A-grade high-rise architecture with expansive open spaces, dedicated lifestyle facilities, and shared township infrastructure.

The development is planned around:

Residential — multiple apartment configurations and layouts.
Open Spaces — approximately 70% reported open area.
Township Infrastructure — a clubhouse of approximately 86,000 sq.ft.
Masterplan — towers, common spaces, internal movement and shared facilities planned as one residential environment.

The scale of the development makes the masterplan and execution an important part of understanding the project.
