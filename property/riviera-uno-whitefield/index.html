About Riviera Uno

Riviera Uno is a 137-villa residential development on Soukya Road in Whitefield, Bengaluru, positioned as an ultra-premium 4 BHK villa community.

The project spans 12 Acres 36.25 Guntas and offers two villa formats — SOL and LUNA — with saleable areas of 3,450 sq.ft. and 3,490 sq.ft. respectively.

The proposition is fundamentally different from apartment-led projects: fewer homes, large-format residences and a villa-focused master plan with landscaped and sports/recreation spaces.

Acre&Key's assessment should go beyond the brochure. The key questions are the actual location experience, legal/title documentation, construction progress, true acquisition cost and the quality of the exact villa being purchased.

INDEPENDENT ADVISORY VERDICT

Acre&Key Verdict

Riviera Uno is an interesting proposition for buyers specifically looking for a large-format villa rather than an apartment.

Its strongest differentiators are the 137-villa format, large 4 BHK homes and the scale of the land parcel.

But this is not a project where the brochure alone should drive the decision.

The buyer should independently evaluate the title documentation, land records, construction timeline, actual access, amenity commitments and the exact villa location before booking.

Whitefield is one of Bengaluru's major employment and residential corridors. Riviera Uno's location on Soukya Road places it within the wider Whitefield ecosystem.
