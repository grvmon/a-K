Legal Due Diligence Notice: According to Indian RERA guidelines, open parking spaces cannot be sold separately by developers. Covered or stilt parking allotments must be explicitly documented in the allotment letter and agreement for sale.

Headline Price vs True All-In Parking Cost

When purchasing an apartment, builders often quote a headline parking price (e.g. ₹3 Lakhs per space). However, GST taxes, registration charges, allocation documentation fees, and mandatory mechanical maintenance funds increase the true out-of-pocket cost.

Understanding the Parking Cost Formula

Your true total parking purchase cost is calculated as:

Base Parking Price = Number of Spaces × Quoted Price per Space

Applicable GST = Base Parking Price × Tax Rate % (5% or 12%)

Total Parking Cost = Base Parking Price + GST + Documentation Fees + Surcharges

Parking Type Structural Features Key Considerations
Covered / Basement Dedicated stilt or basement slot protected from weather. Highest resale value; confirm specific slot number in agreement.
Mechanical / Puzzle Automated hydraulic lift system stacking vehicles vertically. Requires annual AMC maintenance; verify power backup for lifts.
Open Parking Surface parking within society premises. Check RERA compliance regarding builder allotment legality.

5-Step Parking Due Diligence Checklist

Step 01
Verify Allotment Letter

Ensure specific parking slot numbers (e.g. B2-45) are written into your formal allotment letter.

Step 02
Check GST Inclusion

Confirm whether quoted builder parking prices include 5% or 12% GST or if tax is billed extra.

Step 03
Inspect Physical Dimensions

Measure parking slot width and pillar clearance to ensure comfortable SUV maneuvering.

Step 04
Check EV Charging Provision

Verify if electrical conduit or EV charging points are pre-wired to your designated slot.

Step 05
Review Mechanical AMC Terms

If puzzle/mechanical parking is allotted, check resident welfare association AMC maintenance terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a builder sell open parking spaces separately?

Under RERA and Supreme Court rulings (Nahar Builders vs. RWA), open parking spaces form part of common areas and cannot be sold separately by developers.

Does GST apply to parking allotment?

Yes. If parking is purchased as part of an under-construction apartment, GST applies at the standard project rate (typically 5% for non-affordable residential housing).
