How to Calculate Steel Quantity

Estimating steel reinforcement involves determining concrete volume or built-up area, applying a steel consumption rate, and adding a cutting wastage buffer.

Step 1
Calculate Concrete Volume
Multiply Length × Width × Thickness to get concrete volume in cubic metres (or built-up area in sq.ft).
Step 2
Apply Consumption Rate
Multiply volume by the structural consumption rate (e.g. 100 kg/m³ for slabs/beams, 3.5 to 4.5 kg/sq.ft for built-up area).
Step 3
Add Cutting Wastage
Add 5% to 10% wastage for cutting losses, lap splices, and hook bends.
Step 4
Convert to Tonnes
Divide final weight in kilograms by 1,000 to convert to metric tonnes for supplier ordering.

How to Calculate Steel Bar Weight (D²/162)

Unit weight per metre of round steel rebar is calculated using the formula Weight (kg/m) = D² / 162, where D is the diameter in millimetres.

  • • 8 mm Bar: 8² / 162 = 0.395 kg/m
  • • 10 mm Bar: 10² / 162 = 0.617 kg/m
  • • 12 mm Bar: 12² / 162 = 0.889 kg/m
  • • 16 mm Bar: 16² / 162 = 1.580 kg/m
  • • 20 mm Bar: 20² / 162 = 2.469 kg/m
  • • 25 mm Bar: 25² / 162 = 3.858 kg/m

Frequently Asked Questions

How much steel is required for 1 cu.m. of concrete?

Standard residential RCC construction uses approximately 80 to 120 kg of steel per cubic metre of concrete depending on structural load.

How do you calculate the weight of a steel rebar?

Steel rebar weight is calculated using the formula Weight (kg/m) = D²/162, where D is the bar diameter in millimetres.

What is the weight of a 12mm steel bar per metre?

A 12mm TMT steel bar weighs approximately 0.889 kg per metre (12² / 162 = 0.8889 kg/m).
