About Sumadhura Solace

Sumadhura Solace is a premium residential development in Whitefield, positioned around a simple proposition: more space and greenery without giving up access to East Bengaluru's employment hubs.

The project is spread across a large ~11.6-acre parcel and is planned as a two-phase development. The official developer information positions the project around spacious 3 and 4 BHK residences, landscaped open spaces, clubhouse facilities and family-oriented amenities.

acre&key's assessment goes one step further: the project's most interesting attribute is not simply its amenity count. It is the combination of land parcel, reported open area, larger home formats and Whitefield employment connectivity.

That combination makes it worth considering for buyers who intend to stay for several years rather than buyers looking purely for the fastest appreciation or shortest possession timeline.

INDEPENDENT ADVISORY VERDICT

Acre&Key Verdict

Strong End-Use Pick but not a "buy at any price" project.

Sumadhura Solace scores 3.9/5 in Acre&Key's project assessment.

The project makes the most sense for a buyer who values space, greenery, family-oriented living and long-term usability and is comfortable waiting until the targeted December 2029 possession timeline.

The strongest part of the proposition is the project's physical scale: approximately 11.6 acres, a relatively low reported density of ~66.6 homes/acre based on the 777-home research figure, and approximately 81% reported open area.

The trade-off is equally important: this is not a ready-home proposition, not a walk-to-metro proposition and not the obvious choice for an investor seeking a quick exit.

About the Locality

⭐
Whitefield: A mature employment corridor with a traffic problem
Corridor Profile & Demand Drivers

Whitefield has evolved from an IT-focused suburban market into one of Bengaluru's largest integrated residential and employment ecosystems.

Its strongest demand driver remains employment: ITPL, EPIP, ORR and surrounding technology clusters create a large tenant and end-user base.

The locality also benefits from a deep social infrastructure ecosystem — schools, hospitals, malls, restaurants and everyday services.

!The Major Infrastructure Constraint
The major weakness is equally clear: connectivity exists, but travel reliability can be poor during peak hours.

Sumadhura Solace is located on Marathahalli Main Road, Thubarahalli Village, Varthur Hobli, Whitefield, Bengaluru.
What is the starting price for Sumadhura Solace? +
Starting indicative prices begin at ₹2.99 Cr* for 3 BHK (1,705–2,255 sq.ft.) and ₹3.88 Cr* for 4 BHK (2,270–2,730 sq.ft.).
What configurations are available? +
Spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK luxury residences ranging from 1,705 to 2,730 sq.ft. saleable area with ~66-67% carpet efficiency.
What is the possession date? +
The target completion and handover date for Phase 1 is December 2029.
How many homes are there? +
777 total residences across 7 towers on an 11.66-acre land parcel with 81% open space.
What is the total project land area? +
Sumadhura Solace spans 11.66 acres with 81% open space and surrounding twin lake frontage.
Which metro station is nearest to Sumadhura Solace? +
The Whitefield / Hope Farm Metro Station (Purple Line) is approximately ~15 minutes away, providing seamless connectivity across Bengaluru.
Which tech parks are near Sumadhura Solace? +
Major nearby IT hubs include ITPL (~14 mins), Outer Ring Road ORR (~5 mins), Marathahalli (~12 mins), Brigade Tech Gardens, and Prestige Tech Park.
Is Sumadhura Solace good for investment? +

Yes, rated as a Strong End-Use Pick due to 81% open space, 45,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, 90+ lifestyle amenities, and larger home formats.
What are the maintenance charges? +
Maintenance charges will be finalized prior to handover by the builder association (typically ₹4 – ₹5 per sq.ft./month for luxury Whitefield communities).
