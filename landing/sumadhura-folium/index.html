About Sumadhura Folium

A 16.5-acre master-planned residential precinct of ~1,300 homes across 7 high-rise towers in Borewell Road, Core Whitefield, East Bengaluru.

Planned across 7 residential towers, the development balances premium high-rise architecture with expansive open spaces, dedicated lifestyle amenities, and shared community infrastructure.

01 7 Towers

2B + G + 19 floors with 3 & 4 BHK flexible layouts.

02 83% Open Area

Expansive greenery, courtyard lawns & tree avenues.

03 ~54k sq.ft. Club

Dual lifestyle hubs with 40+ resort-grade amenities.

04 16.5-Acre Master

Integrated live-work township with internal transit.

The scale of the development makes the masterplan and execution an important part of understanding the project.
