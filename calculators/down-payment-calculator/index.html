How Is Your Home Down Payment Calculated?

When purchasing property, banks and financial institutions finance a percentage of the total property value known as the Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio. The remaining portion must be funded directly by the buyer as the Minimum Down Payment.

Your down payment and cash requirements are calculated in three simple steps:

Down Payment Formula Breakdown

1
Step 1 — Calculate Maximum Loan Amount Maximum Loan Amount = Property Price × LTV%
2
Step 2 — Calculate Minimum Down Payment Minimum Down Payment = Property Price − Maximum Loan Amount
or: Minimum Down Payment = Property Price × (1 − LTV%)
3
Step 3 — Determine Additional Cash Required Additional Cash Required = MAX( 0, Minimum Down Payment − Available Cash )

RBI Home Loan LTV Limits & Minimum Down Payment Rules

In India, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) mandates statutory Loan-to-Value (LTV) caps on residential housing loans. This determines the maximum amount a bank or NBFC can lend against the property value, directly establishing your minimum upfront down payment:

Property Agreement Value Maximum Bank Loan (LTV) Mandatory Down Payment
Up to ₹30 Lakhs Up to 90% 10% Minimum
₹30 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs Up to 80% 20% Minimum
Above ₹75 Lakhs Up to 75% 25% Minimum

Important Upfront Cash Costs to Keep in Mind

The down payment is only one part of the total liquid cash required when buying property in India. In addition to the minimum down payment, buyers must budget for upfront non-loan expenses (you can also use our Total Cost of Buying a Home Calculator to evaluate total out-of-pocket overheads):

  • Stamp Duty & Registration: Typically 5% to 7% of property value depending on state regulations.
  • GST on Under-Construction Units: 1% for affordable housing or 5% for standard residential units.
  • Legal & Title Verification Fees: Legal consultation and title search fees (₹15,000 - ₹50,000).
  • Interior & Move-in Costs: Interior woodwork, kitchen, and furnishing expenses.

Down Payment Calculator FAQs

What is the minimum down payment required in India?
As per RBI guidelines, banks finance 80% to 90% of the property cost for loans up to ₹30 Lakhs, 80% for loans between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹75 Lakhs, and 75% for loans above ₹75 Lakhs. Thus, minimum down payments range from 10% to 25%.
What is the difference between minimum down payment and additional cash required?
Minimum Down Payment is the total cash needed to cover the unfinanced property value. Additional Cash Required is only the extra shortfall between your available savings and the required down payment.
Can I take a personal loan for the down payment?
Lenders discourage using unsecured personal loans for down payments as it increases your total debt-to-income ratio (FOIR) and negatively impacts home loan approval.
How does higher down payment reduce total interest?
Paying a higher down payment lowers the principal loan amount needed from the bank, directly reducing monthly EMIs and total interest payable over the loan tenure.
