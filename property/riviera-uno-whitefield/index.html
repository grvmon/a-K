About Riviera Uno

Riviera Uno is a 137-villa residential development on Soukya Road in Whitefield, Bengaluru.

The project spans 12 Acres 36.25 Guntas and offers two villa formats — SOL and LUNA — with saleable areas of 3,450 sq.ft. and 3,490 sq.ft.

The proposition is fundamentally different from an apartment-led development: large-format 4 BHK villas within a villa-focused master plan.

acre&key's analysis goes beyond the brochure to evaluate the location, project documentation, construction timeline, actual cost and the exact villa being considered.

acre&key Verdict

Luxury Villa Proposition — Due Diligence Matters

Riviera Uno is an interesting proposition for buyers specifically looking for a large-format villa rather than an apartment.

The project's strongest differentiators are its villa-only format, large 4 BHK homes and 137-villa scale.

The trade-off is that the project is still under construction, the RERA completion timeline extends to January 2030, and the buyer should independently verify title, land records, execution, amenities and the exact villa before committing.
