How Karnataka Calculates Stamp Duty and Registration Fees

In Karnataka, stamp duty and registration charges are statutory taxes governed by the Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957 and the Registration Act, 1908. Rather than charging a single flat rate, Karnataka calculates your government fee in four layers: a base stamp duty, an infrastructure cess, a municipal or gram panchayat surcharge, and a title registration fee.

When purchasing an apartment, villa, or plot in Bengaluru, the charges apply in four steps:

Karnataka Stamp Duty & Registration Formula Breakdown

1
Step 1: Determine the Taxable Consideration

Under Kaveri 2.0 rules, taxable value is the higher of your agreement sale value or the state-notified guidance value.

Taxable Value = MAX( Sale Agreement Value, Kaveri 2.0 Guidance Value )
2
Step 2: Base Stamp Duty (Slab-Based)

Properties above ₹ 45 L attract 5.0% base duty. Properties from ₹ 21 L to ₹ 45 L attract 3.0%, and properties up to ₹ 20 L attract 2.0%.

Base Stamp Duty = Taxable Value × Base Rate% (5.0% for > ₹ 45 L)
3
Step 3: Add Mandatory Cess & Surcharge

Karnataka adds a 10% Infrastructure Cess on the base stamp duty (0.5% of property value) plus a 2% Municipal Surcharge in BBMP areas (0.1% of property value). That brings your effective stamp duty to 5.6%.

Effective Stamp Duty = Base Duty + (10% Cess on Duty) + (2% Surcharge on Duty) = 5.6%
4
Step 4: Add Deed Registration Fee (1.0% Flat)

Sale deed registration in Karnataka has no statutory ceiling. It's a flat 1.0% of the taxable property value.

Total Statutory Outflow = ( Taxable Value × 5.6% ) + ( Taxable Value × 1.0% ) = Taxable Value × 6.6%

Kaveri 2.0 Guidance Value vs Market Price

Buyers often ask how government guidance values affect what they pay. In Karnataka, the Department of Stamps and Registration maintains an official baseline valuation for every survey number and apartment complex on the Kaveri 2.0 portal.

By law, you pay stamp duty on whichever number is higher: your agreed sale consideration or the Kaveri guidance value. For instance, if you agree to buy an apartment for ₹ 1.2 Cr but Kaveri records the guidance value at ₹ 1.25 Cr, your 6.6% charges apply to ₹ 1.25 Cr. If your agreement price is ₹ 1.5 Cr and the guidance value is ₹ 1.2 Cr, you pay on the full ₹ 1.5 Cr.

Does Karnataka Offer a Stamp Duty Discount for Women?

Several northern states like Delhi, Haryana, and Punjab grant a 1% to 2% concession on stamp duty when a woman buys property. Karnataka does not offer any stamp duty discount for female buyers.

Whether you buy individually as a man, a woman, or jointly, the statutory rates remain identical: 5.6% stamp duty and 1.0% registration fee.

Incidental Costs at the Sub-Registrar Office

Beyond the primary 6.6% statutory charges, budget for these administrative and legal expenses during deed registration:

  • SRO Scanning & Archival Charges: Approximately ₹ 300 to ₹ 500 (calculated at ₹ 30 to ₹ 50 per page of the sale deed).
  • E-Stamping Facilitation: Stock Holding Corporation of India (SHCIL) charges approximately ₹ 500 to ₹ 1,000 for generating the digital stamp certificate.
  • Legal Title Due Diligence & Deed Drafting: Property lawyers in Bengaluru charge ₹ 25,000 to ₹ 50,000 to verify 30-year encumbrance certificates, examine parent deeds, and draft the conveyance deed.
  • Khata Transfer / e-Khata Application: BBMP or local panchayat mutation fees (₹ 1,000 to ₹ 3,000) for transferring the municipal Khata into your name.

Tax Deductions Under Section 80C

Under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, you can claim a deduction for stamp duty and registration charges paid towards buying a new residential house. However, three key rules apply:

  • The total deduction under Section 80C is capped at ₹ 1.5 L per financial year, which is shared with EPF, PPF, ELSS, and home loan principal repayments.
  • You can claim this deduction only in the financial year you paid the charges.
  • This benefit applies exclusively under the Old Tax Regime. The New Tax Regime does not offer Section 80C deductions.

Verify with your CA before acting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total stamp duty and registration charge in Bengaluru for 2026?
For residential apartments priced above ₹ 45 L in Bengaluru (BBMP area), total statutory charges are 6.6% of the registered value. This comprises 5.0% base stamp duty, 0.5% infrastructure cess (10% of stamp duty), and 0.1% BBMP surcharge (2% of stamp duty), making effective stamp duty 5.6%, plus a 1.0% registration fee.
Does Karnataka offer a stamp duty discount for female property buyers?
No. Karnataka does not offer concessional stamp duty or registration rates for women. Stamp duty rates apply uniformly whether you buy as a man, woman, or jointly.
Is stamp duty calculated on the agreement value or government guidance value?
Under Kaveri 2.0 rules, stamp duty and registration fees are calculated on whichever amount is higher: the actual sale consideration agreed between buyer and seller, or the government-notified guidance value.
Can stamp duty and registration charges be funded through a home loan?
No. RBI regulations exclude stamp duty, registration charges, and statutory taxes from property cost when computing bank Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits. You must pay this entire 6.6% from your own personal savings.
Can I claim income tax deductions on stamp duty under Section 80C?
Yes. Stamp duty and registration charges qualify for a tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹ 1.5 L. This benefit is available only in the financial year the payment is made, and applies solely under the Old Tax Regime.
What are the stamp duty slabs for properties below ₹ 45 Lakhs in Karnataka?
Properties valued up to ₹ 20 L attract 2% base stamp duty plus 10% cess (effective 2.2%) with 0.5% registration fee. Properties between ₹ 21 L and ₹ 45 L attract 3% base stamp duty plus 10% cess (effective 3.3%) with 1.0% registration fee.
How is stamp duty paid during property registration in Karnataka?
Stamp duty in Karnataka is paid digitally through the Kaveri 2.0 portal using e-stamping (Stock Holding Corporation of India / SHCIL certificate) or online payment gateway before booking the Sub-Registrar Office appointment.
Are there additional incidental costs at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)?
Yes. Beyond stamp duty and the 1% registration fee, buyers pay document scanning fees (₹ 300 to ₹ 500), e-stamping facilitation charges (~₹ 500), and deed drafting/legal representation fees (usually ₹ 25,000 to ₹ 50,000).
