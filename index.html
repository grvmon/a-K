BENGALURU MICRO-MARKET LENS

Bengaluru is not one market.
It is many micro-markets.

A ₹3 Cr home in Whitefield, Hennur, Sarjapur or Devanahalli represents very different risk-return and life-fit decisions.

East

Whitefield / KR Puram / Varthur / ORR / Budhigere Cross
For: Tech professionals seeking rental & established infra.
Watch out for: Project selection and traffic.
Price Range ₹9k-18k / sq ft
Avg. Growth (YoY) +11% YoY

North

Hebbal / Hennur / Thanisandra / Bagalur / Devanahalli
For: Long-term appreciation and airport-driven growth
Watch out for: Infrastructure timing, future built-in pricing.
Price Range ₹8k-16k / sq ft
Avg. Growth (YoY) +6% to +10%

South-East / West / Central

Sarjapur, Electronic city, JP Nagar, Indra nagar
For: Families prioritising schools, CBD and current livability.
Watch out for: Road infrastructure, connectivity and supply
Price Range ₹6.5k-12k / sq ft
Avg. Growth (YoY) +6% to +8%
