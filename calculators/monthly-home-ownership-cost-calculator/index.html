What is the True Monthly Cost of Owning a Home?

When buying a home, most buyers budget exclusively for their monthly home loan EMI. However, owning a property involves several ongoing recurring expenses that continue even after your purchase is complete.

Component 1
Home Loan EMI
Your monthly principal and interest repayment toward your home loan.
Component 2
Society Maintenance
Recurring monthly apartment/gated community charges for common area upkeep, security, and amenities.
Component 3
Property Tax
Annual municipal property tax converted into a monthly equivalent.
Component 4
Home Insurance
Annual property structure and contents insurance premium converted into a monthly equivalent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EMI the only monthly cost of owning a home?

No. Your home loan EMI is only the financing cost. Owning a home also requires ongoing payments for society maintenance, annual property taxes, home insurance premiums, and utility/upkeep expenses.

How is monthly property tax calculated?

Annual property tax assessed by your local municipal corporation is divided by 12 to determine your monthly property tax obligation.

What is included in monthly maintenance?

Monthly maintenance includes apartment or gated community society charges for common area lighting, security, lift operation, clubhouse access, water supply, and general maintenance.

Why are one-time purchase costs excluded from this calculator?

One-time acquisition costs such as stamp duty, registration, legal fees, brokerage, and initial interior work occur upfront at the time of purchase and are not part of your ongoing monthly recurring commitment.
