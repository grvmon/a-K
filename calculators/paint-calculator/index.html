How Much Paint Do I Need?

Estimating room paint requires calculating gross wall area, deducting door and window openings, adding ceiling area, multiplying by number of coats, and applying a manufacturer coverage rate with a wastage buffer.

Step 1
Gross Wall Area
Multiply perimeter by height (2 × (Length + Width) × Height) to calculate gross wall surface area.
Step 2
Deduct Openings
Subtract the combined surface area of doors and windows to arrive at net paintable wall space.
Step 3
Add Ceiling Area
If painting the ceiling, add room length × width to the net paintable wall area.
Step 4
Multiply by Coats
Multiply total paintable area by the number of coats (standard is 2 coats) to calculate required surface coverage.

Before Buying Paint Checklist

  • ✓ Measure room length, width, and clear wall height accurately.
  • ✓ Count all doors and windows and subtract their surface areas.
  • ✓ Confirm if ceiling is to be painted in the same or different color.
  • ✓ Check the manufacturer's coverage rate (typically 90–120 sq.ft/L per coat).
  • ✓ Buy a 10% extra buffer tin from the same batch for future touch-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much paint do I need for a 20x15 ft room?

For a 20x15 ft room with 10 ft ceiling (including ceiling & 2 coats at 100 sq ft/L coverage), you need approximately 20.28 litres of paint including a 10% wastage allowance.

Should I include doors and windows in paint calculations?

No, door and window areas should be deducted from total gross wall area to determine true paintable surface.

How many coats of paint are recommended for interior walls?

Two coats are standard for most interior walls to ensure uniform color depth, hide underlying patches, and improve durability.
