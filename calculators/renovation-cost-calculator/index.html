What Does a Home Renovation Cost?

A complete apartment renovation involves multiple layers: basic civil masonry, flooring replacement, electrical rewiring, plumbing fixture upgrades, modular kitchen installation, painting, and custom carpentry.

Understanding the Renovation Cost Formula

Your total renovation budget is calculated as:

Base Area Cost = Renovation Area × Rate per Sq. Ft.

Subtotal = Base Area Cost + Itemized Components + Labour Charges

Total Investment = Subtotal + Contingency Buffer (10% - 15%)

Renovation Tier Rate / Sq. Ft. Inclusions & Material Quality
Basic Tier ₹800 / sq. ft. Repainting, basic tile repairs, standard electrical fixtures, and minor plumbing.
Standard Tier ₹1,500 / sq. ft. Vitrified flooring, acrylic wall putty painting, modular kitchen, and branded sanitaryware.
Premium Tier ₹2,500+ / sq. ft. Italian marble/hardwood flooring, veneer woodwork, luxury fittings, and false ceiling LED lighting.

5-Step Renovation Due Diligence Checklist

Step 01
Measure Active Scope Area

Distinguish overall apartment area from the specific rooms or zones being renovated.

Step 02
Obtain Itemized Contractor Quotes

Require line-item pricing for civil demolition, tile laying, electrical wiring, and painting per sq. ft.

Step 03
Reserve 10% - 15% Contingency

Unforeseen electrical conduit changes or dampness waterproofing repairs frequently arise mid-project.

Step 04
Structure Milestone Payments

Tie contractor payments to verified completion stages (e.g. 20% advance, 30% after civil, 30% after woodwork, 20% final hand-over).

Step 05
Confirm Society Work Timings

Verify resident association guidelines regarding noisy demolition hours and material elevator access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much contingency buffer should I reserve for renovation?

We recommend reserving 10% to 15% of your subtotal budget. Hidden plumbing leaks, wall dampness repairs, or electrical rewiring updates often emerge after wall demolition starts.

Does modular kitchen cost depend on apartment area?

No. Modular kitchen costs depend primarily on running foot length, carcass material (BWP plywood/HDF), shutter finishes (laminate/acrylic/lacquered glass), and hardware fittings (Blum/Hettich).
