06 · MASTER DESIGN TOKENS & INTERACTION SYSTEM

This section defines the single source of truth for Hero Typography, Cards & Boxes, Imagery, SVG Particle Animations, and Hover Interaction policies used across all pages.

1. Hero Text & Headings

  • Hero H1: Marcellus 400 · clamp(1.8rem, 1.5rem + 1.2vw, 2.4rem) · line-height 1.22
  • Brass Accent: Refined Brass (#8B5A45) Italic 400 (.highlight-brass)
  • Section H2: Marcellus 400 · clamp(1.3rem–2.1rem) · line-height 1.25–1.35
  • Lead / Subtitle: Manrope 500 · 1.05rem–1.15rem · Muted Slate (#59616B)
  • Kicker Bar: 24px × 2px Refined Brass gold line bar (.hero-kicker-line)

2. Cards & Box Tokens

  • Box Fill: Clean White #FFFFFF or Warm Ivory #FAF8F5
  • Border Rule: 1.5px solid #E4DACB (--divider) across all step cards
  • Unified Box Size: 205px min/max width × 64px height
  • Corner Radius: 10px to 12px for luxury architectural framing
  • Gold Numbers: Marcellus 400 · 1.15rem · Antique Brass #8B5A45

3. Imagery & Tone Direction

  • Primary Mood: Calm clarity, self-reliance, and independent confidence
  • Narrative Focus: Home buyer couple reviewing floor plans alone (No stress, no 3rd party contradiction)
  • Photography Specs: Daylight, natural warm tones, 8K editorial resolution
  • Image Framing: 12px border radius, subtle drop shadow 0 6px 20px rgba(31, 43, 56, 0.08)

4. SVG Particle Animations

  • Core Engine: Dynamic SVG bezier wire canvas with traveling glow particles
  • Wire stroke: 1.5px dashed rgba(139, 90, 69, 0.28)
  • Incoming Particles: Muted gold SVG flow dots traveling into core hub
  • Outgoing Particles: Luminous gold particles exiting toward risk & execution outputs

5. Hover & Interaction Policy

  • Stakeholder Hub Boxes: Static non-hover policy (No transform, no border shift on hover)
  • Interactive Cards: Subtle -2px Y-axis lift on interactive process cards
  • Buttons: Magnetic gold badges with 200ms smooth scale transition
  • Focus State: 2px brass focus ring with 3px offset for WCAG accessibility

6. Color & Ratio System

  • Canvas (75%): Warm Ivory #F7F3EC
  • Primary Ink (20%): Deep Navy #1F2B38
  • Accent (5%): Antique Brass #8B5A45
  • Rules: Brass used exclusively for accents, kickers, numbers & dividers

08 · IMAGE & TONE DIRECTION

Use

  • Architectural composition, natural light and tactile materials.
  • Warm, restrained colour grading; real homes and authentic moments.
  • Editorial crops with room for typography and breathing space.
  • Language that sounds like an independent advisor: calm, precise and discreet.

Avoid

  • Generic façades, artificial HDR, stock-smile photography and cluttered collages.
  • Bright gradients, glossy effects, excessive rounded cards or loud promotional badges.
  • Sales-heavy language such as “best deal” or “limited time only”.
  • Using brass for long text, interactive errors or low-contrast labels.

FocusAll links and controls show a 2px brass focus ring with a 3px offset.
