● 3 Unmatched Advantages

1. Phase 2 Entry Ticket Advantage

While Phase 1 (Raintree Park) required an entry threshold of ₹2.75 Cr+ for large 3 & 4 BHKs, Evergreen Phase 2 introduces accessible 1 & 2 BHK configurations starting from ₹1.07 Cr in the exact same 24-acre integrated township.

2. Integrated 24-Acre Scale & 80% Greens

Spread over 24 acres with over 80% open landscaped greenery and permanent lakefront vistas, offering rare master-planned social infrastructure and clubhouse amenities that standalone towers cannot replicate.

3. Blue-Chip Tenant Demand

Located directly opposite the Varthur-Whitefield tech growth corridor, units enjoy exceptional rental liquidity from high-earning tech talent working in ITPL, Bagmane Tech Park, and the ORR corridor.

● 3 Critical Watch-Items

1. Varthur Junction Peak Congestion

The bottleneck between Varthur police station and the lake bridge experiences heavy delays during peak commuting hours (8:30–10:30 AM). While CDP road expansions are underway, buyers must evaluate their daily office trajectory.

2. June 2030 Delivery Horizon

With sanctioned RERA completion in June 2030, this project is ideal for long-term wealth creators and end-users with a 4–5 year horizon, but unsuitable for buyers seeking immediate occupancy.

3. Cauvery Stage V Pipeline Timing

While municipal Cauvery water supply is planned under BWSSB Stage V for this belt, early occupation phases typically rely on managed tanker and deep borewell augmentation.
