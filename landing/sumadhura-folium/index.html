Project Overview: Sumadhura Folium

Sumadhura Folium is a residential community on Borewell Road in Core Whitefield. The project spans approximately 16.5 acres and comprises 7 towers with approximately 1,300 residences.

It offers 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Study, 4 BHK and 4 BHK + Study configurations ranging from approximately 1,515 to 2,435 sq.ft. The project's proposition is centred around Core Whitefield connectivity, ITPL/EPIP access (~3 km), operational Purple Line Metro connectivity (~2–3 km), large open space (83%), and a substantial ~54,000 sq.ft. amenity ecosystem.

Designed for families seeking core employment catchment proximity alongside a high quality of life, Sumadhura Folium provides extensive sporting, wellness, and leisure facilities within a self-contained community environment.
