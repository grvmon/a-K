How to Calculate Flooring Cost

Estimating your complete flooring budget involves calculating net room area, adding cutting wastage, calculating material purchase cost, and incorporating installation labour and skirting.

Step 1
Calculate Floor Area
Multiply Room Length × Room Width to obtain the total floor area in square feet.
Step 2
Add Cutting Wastage
Add 10% (straight layout) or 15% (diagonal/herringbone layout) to account for wall cuts and tile breakage.
Step 3
Material Cost
Multiply total material required (with wastage) by the supplier price per sq. ft.
Step 4
Add Installation & Skirting
Add contractor installation labour per sq. ft. and perimeter border skirting cost to derive the total all-in budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does tile flooring cost per sq. ft. in India?

Vitrified tile flooring in India typically costs ₹60 to ₹150 per sq. ft. for material and ₹25 to ₹45 per sq. ft. for installation labour.

Why should I add wastage to my flooring calculation?

Wastage (typically 10%) accounts for tile cutting along walls, diagonal cuts, breakage during transport, and future repair spares.

What is the cost of skirting in flooring?

Skirting installation typically costs ₹30 to ₹60 per running foot including material and border cutting.
