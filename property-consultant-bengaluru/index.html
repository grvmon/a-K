BUYER ADVISORY FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Property Consultants in Bengaluru

Clear answers on how property consultancy works, fee models, due diligence scope, and buyer representation.

What does a property consultant in Bengaluru do?

A property consultant in Bengaluru helps buyers evaluate the residential market, compare competing communities, and acquire property on fair terms. Unlike typical brokers who focus on facilitating a single sale, a consultant conducts comprehensive requirement mapping, K-RERA due diligence, builder track record audits, total acquisition cost arithmetic, and transaction negotiation support.

How do I choose the right property consultant in Bengaluru?

Look for a property consultant who demonstrates deep micro-market knowledge across your commute corridor, maintains a structured due diligence framework, and provides full transparency regarding their commercial model. Ask to see sample evaluation dossiers and verify that their advice is structured around your family's needs rather than specific developer sales quotas.

What is the difference between a property consultant and a real estate broker?

A broker primarily acts as an intermediary to execute a transaction and sell developer inventory. A buyer-first property consultant focuses on your overall purchase decision, challenging marketing claims, checking legal approvals, calculating total all-in cost, and evaluating whether a property represents sound long-term livability and value before you commit.

Does acre&key charge buyers an advisory fee?

No. acre&key operates with a ₹0 advisory fee for homebuyers. We provide complete advisory, project audits, site visit assistance, and negotiation support without charging buyers a consulting fee. Any standard industry referral arrangements are fully disclosed on request.

Can a property consultant help negotiate the property price with developers?

Yes. Based on comparative micro-market transaction history, developer inventory velocity, and payment schedule flexibility, acre&key assists buyers with evidence-backed commercial negotiation strategies to secure optimal pricing, reduced floor rise, or tailored milestone structures.

Can an investment consultant estimate rental yields and capital growth?

A property consultant can model projected rental yield, tenant profile demand, competing upcoming supply, and total maintenance overheads using historical absorption data.
